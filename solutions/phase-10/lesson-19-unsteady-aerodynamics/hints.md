# P10.19  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. ω = 2πf, and b is half the chord. For the rotor, a once-per-revolution pitch change has ω = Ω = V_tip/R, and the local speed at 0.75 R is 0.75 V_tip; the radius cancels out of k except through the ratio b/r.
2. (a) For α₃/₄ = e^{iks}, dα₃/₄/dσ = ik e^{ikσ}; substitute τ = s − σ and pull e^{iks} out of the integral. (b) The Laplace transform of e^{−βs} is 1/(p + β). (c) φ′ decays exponentially, φ does not. (d) Solve ½e^{−βs} = 0.1.
3. A once-per-revolution oscillation completes 360° of azimuth per cycle, so a phase angle of the lift relative to the pitch is directly an azimuth angle.
4. The pivot enters the effective angle at the three-quarter chord through (½ − a). For (b), the time-domain run and Theodorsen differ by two things: the discretisation (tiny) and Jones's fit of the Wagner function (about 1–2 %).
5. Δn = ΔL/W with ΔL = ½ρV² S CL_α (w/V) times the unsteady reduction factor; w/V is the gust angle. A gust many chords long changes slowly compared with the time the wake needs to settle.
6. The circulatory lift acts at the quarter chord, so its moment about the pivot is (a + ½) times it; ask what sign the work of that moment has when the lift lags the motion.
