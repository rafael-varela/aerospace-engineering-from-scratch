# P10.20  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. The images sit 2h below the wake, with opposite signs. At the centre, each image's velocity points along the line from it; only its vertical component counts, a fraction (b_v/2)/r of the total with r² = (b_v/2)² + (2h)².
2. (a) By symmetry the two vortices' velocities at a point of the plane are mirror images of each other; with opposite circulation their vertical parts cancel. (c) The tangency condition fixes the total normal velocity; if the induced downwash is smaller, the circulation must be larger to cancel V sin α.
3. Use the same calls as the lesson with b = 60, c = 7. Holding the speed means holding CL; ask what a smaller K does to the induced drag and what a larger CL_α does to α.
4. The lift is the weight, so CL = W/(½ρV²S); the induced drag at that CL is K times its free-air value. A drag change ΔD at constant thrust is a deceleration change ΔD/m.
5. For (a) remember that the formula assumes an elliptic load: what is the downwash at the centre of a rectangular wing's wake compared with its average? For (c), ΔCm ≈ −V_H a_t Δα_t with Δα_t = −Δε.
6. A thick wing near the ground makes a converging-diverging channel under itself. A ground board's boundary layer moves with the board, the real runway does not move relative to the air ahead of the wheels.
