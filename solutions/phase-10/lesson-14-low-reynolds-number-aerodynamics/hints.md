# P10.14  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. `section_cd_friction(Re, Re_tr=1e12)` gives the all-laminar floor. Compare your Re with the 0012's natural-transition Re of 4.2 × 10⁵.
2. (a) ∫₀ˢ s^{5m} ds = s^{5m+1}/(5m + 1). (b) Write θ² = ν·F(s) with F independent of ν. (d) On [0, h], Ue⁵ = a⁵s⁵; compare h(0 + a⁵h⁵)/2 with a⁵h⁶/6.
3. ν = μ/ρ is large on Mars. A Re of order 10⁴ is far below the range where any bubble closes.
4. The 4402 at cl = 0.8 is well above its design angle: look at the leading-edge peak.
5. `ladson_blocks`, `clmax_vs_re`, `cd_at_zero_lift`; pair blocks whose Re differ by less than 10 %. A turbulator forces transition before laminar separation.
6. Re_min is proportional to N_ℓ; the separation point and ΔCp_rec are not affected by it.
