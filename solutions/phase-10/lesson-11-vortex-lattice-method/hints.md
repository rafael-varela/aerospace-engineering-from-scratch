# P10.11  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. The observed order from three lattices needs no exact answer; compare where the uniform and the Hough sequences are heading.
2. (a) The downwash of a 2-D vortex at distance d is Γ/(2πd). (b) Each control point sees both vortices. (c) The lattice force has F_z = L cos α.
3. Forward sweep loads the root; think about what bending does to the incidence of a swept-forward tip.
4. With order p, the extrapolated value is f₃ + (f₃ − f₂)/(2^p − 1).
5. A rectangular wing of AR 1.34 at moderate angle has strong tip vortices that leave the side edges.
6. Ask what a span efficiency above 1 means for a flat wing.
