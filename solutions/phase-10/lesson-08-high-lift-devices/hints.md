# P10.08  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. cos θ_h = 2E − 1; τ = 1 − (θ_h − sin θ_h)/π. For the approximation, put θ_h = π − φ and expand sin φ.
2. (a) ∫_{θ_h}^{π} cos nθ dθ = −sin(nθ_h)/n. (b) Differentiate sin θ (1 − cos θ). (d) With the hinge at the leading edge the flap is the whole plate.
3. H = c_h q c_f² b_t with c_f the elevator chord and b_t the span; a positive H pushes the trailing edge down.
4. Rotating the slat nose-down lowers its own angle of attack: watch which element's peak grows.
5. The margin is V_app/V_s = 30/√(2W/(ρ S CL_max)); the needed CL_max is fixed by the margin, not by the flap.
6. Ask what the boundary layer does behind a sharp hinge at 30°, and what the fresh boundary layer on a slotted flap can tolerate.
