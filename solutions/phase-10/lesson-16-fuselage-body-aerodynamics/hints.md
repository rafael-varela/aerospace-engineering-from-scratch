# P10.16  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. A spheroid is closed: its normal force is zero and its moment is a pure couple. Vol = (π/6)LD².
2. (a) Follow a slab of air past the body: it carries vertical momentum ρS·w and the body must change it at the rate V·d/dx. (c) For a cone S ∝ x², so dS/dx ∝ x: the loading grows linearly.
3. dM/dα (exact) = 2q·Vol(k₂ − k₁) for small α; a fin of area S_f at arm l gives a restoring dM/dα = q·a_f·S_f·l.
4. Scale the profile with `trainer_fuselage_radius(x * 7.0 / L)` on a grid from 0 to L; the wing quarter chord stays at x = 2.3 m.
5. The fineness optimum comes from the form factor and the wetted area; the skin friction changes only slowly with Re.
6. A thick tail-cone boundary layer partly separates; the wing's upwash increases the local α of the forward fuselage.
