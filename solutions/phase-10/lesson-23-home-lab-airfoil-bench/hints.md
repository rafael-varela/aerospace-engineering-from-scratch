# P10.23  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. q = ½ρV²; a water column h supports ρ_w g h; along a tube inclined at θ the reading is h/sin θ. A force F in grams-force is F/g × 1000.
2. (a) The pressure difference equals the weight per unit area of the vertical height difference, whatever the tube's slope. (b) Take moments about the pivot. (c) Propagate the absolute uncertainty: ∂C/∂F = 1/(qS).
3. Re = ρVc/μ with sea-level air. Thwaites' λ, and so the separation point, does not depend on Re; Michel's criterion does.
4. Read the generator's docstring for what it assumed; the recorded incline and temperature differ from the truth on purpose.
5. u(CL)² = (u_L/(qS))² + (CL u_q/q)²; u_q² = u_man² + (2εq)² + (0.015q)²/n. At α = 0 the lift is nearly zero, so its relative uncertainty is not useful: compare absolute terms.
6. You can change the fan speed (and so Re) without moving anything in the rig, and you can turn the model upside down.
