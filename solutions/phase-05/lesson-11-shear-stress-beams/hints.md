# P05.11  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. The maximum is 1.5 V/A at the neutral axis; at a quarter depth from the top, y = h/4 from the centroid and the parabola gives 3/4 of the maximum. For the round pin use Q = 2r³/3 and a cut width of 2r.
2. (a) The bending stresses on the two ends of the part differ only through dM; the shear stress on the cut is the only other axial force. (b) Start each half-flange at its free tip; in the flange y is constant, so ∫ty ds is linear in s. (c) Each flange's force is the area under its triangular flow; the two flange forces form a couple.
3. A shear flow along a straight leg acts along that leg. If every leg passes through one point, what moment do the flows have about it? For (b), equal legs make I_xx = I_yy, and the product of inertia is negative when the legs lie along +x and +y from the corner.
4. The cap width enters I and Q_cap; the joint flow uses Q of the whole cap about the neutral axis, (h − t_f)/2 × b_f t_f.
5. For (c) list the segments as: top lip (from its free edge to the flange tip), top flange, web, bottom flange, bottom lip (from the flange tip to its free edge). The lips add flow to the flange before it reaches the web.
6. τ_cr scales as t², the applied τ as 1/t: their ratio scales as 1/t³. A buckled web pulls diagonally on the caps and pushes on the stiffeners.
