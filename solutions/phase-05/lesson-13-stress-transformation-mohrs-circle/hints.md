# P05.13  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Plot the x face at (σ_x, τ_xy) and the y face at (σ_y, −τ_xy); the line between them is a diameter, and its midpoint on the σ axis is the centre. The angle on the circle is twice the angle in the body. Check the sense of rotation with equation (1) at one angle.
2. (a) Resolve the forces on the three faces of the wedge along the normal and along the cut, remembering that each face force is stress times that face's length. (b) Write (σ_x − σ_y)/2 = R cos φ₀ and τ_xy = R sin φ₀. (c) Expand the determinant along a row; for the cubic, use cos 3α = 4cos³α − 3cos α and match coefficients.
3. A weld line at β to the axis has its normal at β + 90°: put that angle into (1). The weld carries no shear only if it lies along a principal direction.
4. The principal stresses do not depend on the axes, but the plane (1, 1, 1)/√3 does: it is the octahedral plane only in principal axes.
5. Write (8) for the three gauge angles and solve the 3 × 3 system; for the closed forms, cos²60° = cos²120° = 1/4 and the sin cos terms have opposite signs. With σ_z = 0, Hooke's law gives ε_z = −ν(ε_x + ε_y)/(1 − ν).
6. A crack runs perpendicular to σ₁. With pressure alone σ₁ is hoop. When σ_x > σ_y, where on Mohr's circle can the σ₁ direction be?
