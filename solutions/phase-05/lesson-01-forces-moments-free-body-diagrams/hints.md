# P05.01  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Write the cross product as the determinant with rows (x̂, ŷ, ẑ), r and F. For the axis moments, normalise the axis vector before the dot product. A cross product is perpendicular to both of its factors.
2. (a) Move P along the axis by λû and expand the cross product. (b) Substitute r_i − B = (r_i − A) + (A − B) in the moment sum. (c) In a plane, M_O is perpendicular to R; ask which point P makes (P − O) × R equal to M_O.
3. Use axes along and across the rail. The buttons push only across the rail, the stop only along it: three unknowns, three equations. Moments about the stop remove the stop force and leave two button forces; ΣF across the rail closes it.
4. Raking the leg moves the vertical ground force off the trunnion: V now has a lever about A. The brace is idle when the line of the ground force passes through A.
5. (a) With y = s sin u, dy = s cos u du and √(1 − sin²u) = cos u. (b) The centroid of a trapezoid is the centroid of a rectangle plus a triangle. (c) Scale each distribution to 5000 N before averaging.
6. For each question, redraw the free-body diagram with the new feature and ask which forces or levers change.
