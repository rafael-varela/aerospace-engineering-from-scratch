# P07.02  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Per kilogram, p₁v₁ = R T₁ whatever p₁ is. Δu depends only on T₂; q follows from Δu + w. For the bottle, m cv = p₀V/((γ − 1)T₀).
2. (a) Expand d(mu)/dt and use dm/dt = −ṁ. (b) Write ρV at the throat in terms of p₀, T₀ and M, and set M = 1. (c) Substitute p = p₀ r^γ and T = T₀ r^(γ−1) into V dρ/dt = −ṁ and separate variables. (d) What is d/dt of U + H_out − Q_in?
3. With m₁ = 0, the filling energy balance is m₂u₂ = m₂h_line. For (c), m₂ = p₂V/(R T₂) makes the balance one equation in T₂.
4. τ scales with 1/A. The closed forms are in 2.5 of the lesson; the stop event gives the time.
5. m₀ cv = p₀V/((γ − 1)T₀) for each gas. Compare that, divided by hA, with τ.
6. Ask: is the gas dense, is it uniform, is the wall a constant-temperature reservoir, is the gas still a gas?
