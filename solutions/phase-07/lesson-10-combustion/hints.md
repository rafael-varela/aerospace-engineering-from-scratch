One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Balance C, then H, then O; the nitrogen rides along at 3.727 mol per mole of O₂. The water's partial pressure is its mole fraction times the total pressure.
2. (b) The two heating values differ only in the state of the water. (c) Insert the products at 298.15 K as an intermediate state. (d) Look at what h depends on for an ideal gas.

4. `phi_for_temperature("Jet-A(g)", T4, "air", 298.15, T3)`; for (c) perturb φ by ±10⁻⁴.
5. Evaluate reactants and products at the same temperature: h_fuel(T) + a h_O2(T) − [x h_CO2(T) + (y/2) h_H2O(T)].
6. Compare each temperature with where dissociation starts (about 2000 K); for (b) count the atoms with `complete_products`.
