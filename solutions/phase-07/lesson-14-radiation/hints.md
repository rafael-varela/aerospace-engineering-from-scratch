# P07.14  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. The bottom and the mouth are equal coaxial disks h apart; the side is the only surface that can see itself. The mouth sees the side exactly as the bottom does (symmetry).
2. The kernel cos θ₁ cos θ₂/(πs²) is symmetric in the two surfaces. For ΣQ, sum A_i(J_i − Σ_j F_ij J_j) and use reciprocity to swap the order of the double sum.
3. In steady state the bead gains by convection exactly what it loses by radiation; the duct is large, so the bead behaves like a small body in a large enclosure.
4. A shared-edge view factor converges at first order, so F_∞ ≈ 2F_(2n) − F_n. The array's second edge vector lies in the plane normal to the hinge, at angle φ from the radiator's width direction.
5. Radiation leaving a wall may hit another wall several times before it escapes, and each hit is another chance to be absorbed and re-emitted at 400 K.
6. Ask for each step whether it uses the right wavelength band, the right temperature, the right time in the mission, and the right geometry.
