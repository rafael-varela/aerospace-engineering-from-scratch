# P01.07  Method outlines

The steps, without final numbers. Read `worked-solutions.md` only after you have an answer of your own.

## 1.
1. $|\mathbf r| = \sqrt{r_1^2 + r_2^2 + r_3^2}$, same for $\mathbf v$. 2. $\mathbf h$ from the three cyclic components of (3); $|\mathbf h|$. 3. $\hat{\mathbf n} = \mathbf h/|\mathbf h|$; $i = \arccos(\hat n_3)$. 4. $v_r = \mathbf v \cdot \hat{\mathbf r}$, $v_t = |\mathbf v - v_r\hat{\mathbf r}|$, $\gamma = \operatorname{atan2}(v_r, v_t)$; check $|\mathbf h| = |\mathbf r| v_t$. 5. $p = |\mathbf h|^2/\mu$. 6. $\mathbf a = -\mu\mathbf r/r^3$; $a_t = \mathbf a \cdot \hat{\mathbf v}$, $a_n = |\mathbf a \times \hat{\mathbf v}|$; $R = v^2/a_n$. 7. Compare each with `specific_angular_momentum`, `orbital_plane`, `flight_path_angle`, `semi_latus_rectum`, `tangential_normal_components`.

## 2.
(a) Write $|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|^2 = (a_2 b_3 - a_3 b_2)^2 + (a_3 b_1 - a_1 b_3)^2 + (a_1 b_2 - a_2 b_1)^2$ and $(\mathbf a \cdot \mathbf b)^2 = \sum a_i^2 b_i^2 + 2\sum_{i<j} a_i b_i a_j b_j$. The cross terms $-2a_2b_3a_3b_2$ etc. cancel against the $+2a_ib_ia_jb_j$ terms; the squares that remain are exactly the nine terms of $(\sum a_i^2)(\sum b_j^2)$. Then $|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|^2 = |\mathbf a|^2|\mathbf b|^2(1 - \cos^2\theta)$ with $\theta \in [0, \pi]$ so $\sin\theta \ge 0$. (b) $\mathbf a \cdot (\mathbf b \times \mathbf c) = a_1(b_2c_3 - b_3c_2) + a_2(b_3c_1 - b_1c_3) + a_3(b_1c_2 - b_2c_1)$ is the cofactor expansion of the determinant along row 1; row swaps flip a determinant's sign; a cyclic permutation is two swaps. (c) First component of $\mathbf r \times \mathbf v$ is $r_2v_3 - r_3v_2$; differentiate: $\dot r_2 v_3 + r_2\dot v_3 - \dot r_3 v_2 - r_3\dot v_2 = (v_2v_3 - v_3v_2) + (r_2a_3 - r_3a_2)$; same for the others. With $\mathbf a = f(r)\mathbf r$, $\mathbf r \times \mathbf a = f(r)\,\mathbf r \times \mathbf r = \mathbf 0$ because a scalar factor pulls out of a cross product.

## 3.
(a) $\mathbf w = 30\,\text{kt} \times (\sin 210°, \cos 210°, 0)$ in m/s. (b) Heading $\psi$: air velocity $V(\sin\psi, \cos\psi, 0)$; north component of the sum zero gives $\cos\psi = -w_y/V$; east component gives the ground speed; crab angle from `angle_between(air, ground)`. The rejection of $\mathbf w$ from the track direction is the crosswind the heading must cancel, the projection is the headwind that reduces ground speed. (c) Air velocity $(V_h\sin\psi, V_h\cos\psi, 3)$ with $V_h = \sqrt{V^2 - 9}$; redo (b); climb angle $\arcsin(3/|\mathbf v_g|)$; angle between the two velocity vectors with `angle_between`. (d) Along-track progress is a projection (dot); cross-track offset is a rejection or $|\mathbf d \times \hat{\mathbf t}|$ (cross).

## 4.
(a) $M_x = \int_0^s y\,l(y)\,\mathrm{d}y$: triangular $\to (L/s)(s^2/2 - s^2/3)$; uniform $\to (L/2s)(s^2/2)$; elliptic from the lesson. Build each distribution at strip centres, multiply by $\Delta y$, feed `resultant`. (b) Loop over $n$; error $= M_x(n) - M_x^{\text{exact}}$; order $= \log_2(\text{err}_n/\text{err}_{2n})$. Cosine edges: `edges = s*np.sin(np.pi*np.arange(n+1)/(2*n))`, centres and widths from them. (c) Read off the smallest $n$ whose relative error is below $10^{-3}$ in each table.

## 5.
(a) Rigid wing: a tip deflection changes the geometry and (for a swept wing) the local incidence by washout $\approx$ deflection $\times\sin\Lambda/$ span-scale; estimate the lift shift inboard. (b) Compute $a$, $e$, $r_p$; compare the perigee altitude with the atmosphere; the no-drag assumption. (c) Propagate 1 m/s through $\mathbf h$: $\delta i \approx \delta v\,|\mathbf r|/|\mathbf h|$ radians at worst; count how many decimals survive. (d) The sign is fixed by the right-hand rule once the axes are fixed; a positive $M_y$ would be nose-up torsion; swept-back wings with lift aft of the root twist nose-down. A convention that flips the answer also flips which spar cap is in tension.

## 6.
(a) `plane_from_points(p1, p2, p3)`; $d$ is the distance of the plane from the origin; for a two-body orbit the plane contains the attracting centre. (b) $i = \arccos(\hat n_3)$ (take $\hat{\mathbf n}$ with positive $z$; the sign of $\hat{\mathbf n}$ from three points depends on their order). (c) `angle_between(p1, p2)`, `angle_between(p2, p3)`; with a 10-minute spacing, $T = 600 \times 360°/\Delta\theta$ — or use $|\mathbf p|$ and $T = 2\pi\sqrt{r^3/\mu}$ and compare. (d) Station vector $R_E(\cos\phi\cos\lambda, \cos\phi\sin\lambda, \sin\phi)$; `point_plane_distance`; overhead passes need the station to lie in the plane at some time, which rotation about $z$ allows only if the station's latitude is below the inclination. (e) Perturb $\mathbf p_2$ by 1 km along $x$, $y$, $z$; measure the angle between the perturbed and original normals; do the same for $\mathbf v \pm 1$ m/s in `orbital_plane`; compare.
