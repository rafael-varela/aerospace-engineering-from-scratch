# P01.09  Method outlines

The steps, without final numbers. Read `worked-solutions.md` only after you have an answer of your own.

## 1.
1. $K = 1/(\pi e\,\mathrm{AR})$. 2. $V_{md} = \sqrt{2W/\rho S}\,(K/C_{D0})^{1/4}$ (5); $D_{\min} = 2W\sqrt{KC_{D0}}$; $(L/D)_{\max} = W/D_{\min}$. 3. $C_L = 2W/(\rho V_{md}^2S)$; compare with $\sqrt{C_{D0}/K}$. 4. At $1.2V_{md}$: $q = \tfrac12\rho V^2$, parasite $qSC_{D0}$, induced $KW^2/(qS)$; gradient from (2) and (3) with $W' = 0$. 5. Directional derivative: normalise $(1, 0.1)$, dot with the gradient; maximum is $|\nabla D|$. 6. `Aircraft(S=14, cd0=0.028, e=0.8, W0=W, c_w=0.0)`, then `minimum_drag_speed(W, 8, ac)`, `drag`, `drag_gradient`, `directional_derivative`.

## 2.
(a) Differentiate (1) with AR fixed ($K$, $W$ constants) for $D_V$; with $V$ fixed, use the product rule on $KW^2$: $(KW^2)' = K'W^2 + 2KWW'$ with $K' = -K/\mathrm{AR}$, $W' = 2c_w\mathrm{AR}$. (b) $D_V = 0 \Rightarrow \rho VSC_{D0} = 4KW^2/(\rho V^3S) \Rightarrow qSC_{D0} = KW^2/(qS)$; add the two equal parts. (c) $D_{\min} = 2\sqrt{C_{D0}/\pi e}\,(W_0\mathrm{AR}^{-1/2} + c_w\mathrm{AR}^{3/2})$; differentiate, set to zero. (d) $D = AV^2 + B/V^2$: $D_{VV} = 2A + 6B/V^4$ with $B = AV_{md}^4$; $D_{V,\mathrm{AR}} = -2B'/V^3$ and $B' \propto (KW^2)' = 0$ at AR*; $D_{\mathrm{AR,AR}}$ by differentiating the expression in (c) twice and substituting $W_0 = 3c_w\mathrm{AR}^{*2}$. Eigenvalues are the diagonal entries; both positive. (e) $D = qS(C_{D0} + KC_L^2)$, $L = qSC_L$; $D_V = \lambda L_V$ and $D_{C_L} = \lambda L_{C_L}$; the second gives $\lambda = 2KC_L$; substitute in the first. Then $\mathrm{d}(2W\sqrt{KC_{D0}})/\mathrm{d}W = 2\sqrt{KC_{D0}}$.

## 3.
(a) $w_x = -(2x/R^2)w$, $w_y = -(2y/R^2)w$; direction $-(x, y)/r$ toward the core; rate $|\nabla w| = (2r/R^2)w$. (b) $\hat{\mathbf u} = (1, 1)/\sqrt2$; $D_{\hat{\mathbf u}}w = \nabla w \cdot \hat{\mathbf u}$. (c) Heading of $\nabla w$ from north: $\mathrm{atan2}(w_x, w_y)$; turn = that minus 45°. For a non-radial field (e.g. an elliptical thermal $\exp[-(x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2)]$) the gradient is not along the position vector. (d) $w_{xx} = (-2/R^2 + 4x^2/R^4)w$, $w_{yy}$ likewise, $w_{xy} = (4xy/R^4)w$; at the core $H = -(2w_0/R^2)I$; at the glider compute and diagonalise. (e) Circling samples $w$ around a ring; the phase of the sinusoidal variation of $w$ around the circle gives the direction of $\nabla w$ and its amplitude gives $|\nabla w|\times$ radius, because to first order $w(\text{ring}) = w_0 + r\,\nabla w\cdot\hat{\mathbf u}(\theta)$.

## 4.
(a)–(b) Loop over `step`, call `gradient_descent(f, g, x0, step=s, tol=..., max_iter=50000)`, record `len(path) - 1`. (c) `hessian_fd(f, x_opt, [1e-2, 1e-3])` and the same for `scaled_drag` with `[1e-4, 1e-4]`; `np.linalg.eigvalsh`. Count $\approx \ln(e_0/\mathrm{tol})/(s\lambda_{\min})$ for small $s$; near the limit the error factor $|1 - s\lambda_{\max}|$ approaches 1 in the stiff direction and the backtracking halves $s$. (d) Scaled Hessian entries: $H_{VV}V_{\mathrm{ref}}^2/D_{\mathrm{ref}}$, $H_{\mathrm{AR,AR}}\mathrm{AR}_{\mathrm{ref}}^2/D_{\mathrm{ref}}$; set equal and solve for $\mathrm{AR}_{\mathrm{ref}}/V_{\mathrm{ref}}$. (e) `newton_minimize` returns the path; compute $|\nabla f|$ along it; order from $\ln e_{k+1}/\ln e_k$ or by checking $e_{k+1}/e_k^2$ constant.

## 5.
(a) $\delta\mathrm{AR}^*/\mathrm{AR}^* = \tfrac12\delta c_w/c_w$; $\delta D^*/D^* = \sqrt{(\tfrac14\delta c_w/c_w)^2 + (\tfrac12\delta e/e)^2}$. Verify with `optimum_analytic(Aircraft(c_w=..., e=...))`. (b) `drag(minimum_drag_speed(weight(AR), AR), AR)` at 8 and 13. (c) $C(\mathrm{AR}) = 15D_{\min}(\mathrm{AR}) + 4000\max(\mathrm{AR} - 9, 0)$; evaluate the slope of the drag term at 9; if $15|\mathrm{d}D_{\min}/\mathrm{d}\mathrm{AR}| < 4000$ the minimum is at the kink. (d) Prose.

## 6.
(a) `minimum_drag_speed(weight(6.25), 6.25)`, `drag`, `weight`; subtract $D^*$. (b) Central difference of $D_{\min}(\mathrm{AR})$; closed form from 2(c): $2\sqrt{C_{D0}/\pi e}\,(-\tfrac12W_0\mathrm{AR}^{-3/2} + \tfrac32c_w\mathrm{AR}^{1/2})$; times $2b/S$. (c) $\mathcal L = D_{\min}(\mathrm{AR}) + \mu(\mathrm{AR} - 6.25)$, $\mathrm{d}\mathcal L/\mathrm{d}\mathrm{AR} = 0 \Rightarrow \mu = -D_{\min}'(6.25) > 0$; $\mu$ is the drag saved per unit of constraint relaxation. (d) Estimate $\Delta D \approx \lambda_W\Delta W + (\mathrm{d}D_{\min}/\mathrm{d}b)\Delta b$ with $\lambda_W = 2\sqrt{K(6.25)C_{D0}}$; exact: `Aircraft(W0=13150)` at $\mathrm{AR} = 121/16$.
