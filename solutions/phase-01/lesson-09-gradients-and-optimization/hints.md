# P01.09  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. With $c_w = 0$ the weight is constant, so $\partial D/\partial\mathrm{AR}$ has only the $K' = -K/\mathrm{AR}$ term and is always negative. At $V_{md}$ the two drag parts are equal; at $1.2V_{md}$ the parasite part is $1.44\times$ its value at $V_{md}$ and the induced part $1/1.44\times$. The largest directional derivative is $|\nabla D|$, along the gradient itself.
2. Write $D = AV^2 + B(\mathrm{AR})/V^2$ with $A = \tfrac12\rho SC_{D0}$ and $B = 2KW^2/(\rho S)$. Then $D_V = 2AV - 2B/V^3$, $D_{VV} = 2A + 6B/V^4$, and at $V_{md}$ you know $B$ in terms of $A$ and $V^4$. For the cross term note $D_{V,\mathrm{AR}} \propto B'(\mathrm{AR})$, and $B' = 0$ is the AR-optimality condition. For (e) the second Lagrange equation gives $\lambda$ at once.
3. $\nabla w = -(2/R^2)\,w\,(x, y)$: the gradient of a radial Gaussian points straight at the core. A heading of north-east is the unit vector $(1, 1)/\sqrt2$ in $(x, y)$. The Hessian of a Gaussian changes sign at $r = R/\sqrt2$; the glider at $r = 361$ m is outside that ring in the radial direction but not in the tangential one.
4. For a quadratic with Hessian eigenvalue $\lambda$ along some direction, a fixed step $s$ multiplies the error in that direction by $|1 - s\lambda|$; convergence needs $s < 2/\lambda_{\max}$ and the slow direction is $\lambda_{\min}$. The count is roughly $\ln(\mathrm{tol}/e_0)/\ln|1 - s\lambda_{\min}|$. Scaling by the reference values multiplies the Hessian entries by $V_{\mathrm{ref}}^2/D_{\mathrm{ref}}$ and $\mathrm{AR}_{\mathrm{ref}}^2/D_{\mathrm{ref}}$: choose the ratio of the references to equalise the two eigenvalues.
5. $\mathrm{AR}^* \propto c_w^{-1/2}$ and does not depend on $e$ at all; $D^* = 2W^*\sqrt{K^*C_{D0}}$ with $W^* = \tfrac43 W_0$ fixed, so $D^* \propto (K^*)^{1/2} \propto c_w^{1/4}e^{-1/2}$. For (c), the cost function has a kink at AR = 9; check the slope of the drag term there before assuming an interior minimum.
6. The constraint is active, so the answer is the fixed-AR minimum at 6.25. $\mathrm{d}D_{\min}/\mathrm{d}b = (\mathrm{d}D_{\min}/\mathrm{d}\mathrm{AR})(2b/S)$. For the inequality constraint the multiplier must be non-negative in the convention $\nabla f + \mu\nabla g = 0$; check which sign you get and what it says about the direction the constraint pushes. For (d) the linear estimate is $\lambda_W\Delta W + \lambda_b\Delta b$ with $\lambda_W = 2\sqrt{KC_{D0}}$ evaluated at AR = 6.25, not at AR*.
