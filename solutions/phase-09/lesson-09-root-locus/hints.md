# P09.09  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Changing T moves only the pole at −1/T; the cubic's Routh condition is a₂a₁ > a₃a₀.
2. (c) D/N = s^{n−m}(1 − (Σp − Σz)/s + …) ≈ (s − σ_a)^{n−m} for large s. (d) On the real axis K(s) = −D/N; differentiate.
3. With a right-half-plane open-loop pole, the closed loop is unstable at small K; look for where the branch from +2 crosses the imaginary axis.
4. A slow real closed-loop pole near a zero contributes a small, slow term to the step response; how small depends on how close it is to the zero.
5. (a) and (e) are regular (e is a triple root); (b) and (d) put a lone zero in the first column (use ε); (c) produces a whole row of zeros (use the auxiliary polynomial).
6. The loop gain is K·V, so a change of V acts like a change of K along the same locus; for the inner lag, look at where the pole −1/τ sits relative to the pair.
