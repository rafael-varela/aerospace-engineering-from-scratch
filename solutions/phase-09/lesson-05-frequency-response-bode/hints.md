# P09.05  Hints

One hint per problem. Read only the one you need, then `method.md` if the hint is not enough.

1. K/(s(τs + 1)) = K · s⁻¹ · (1 + s/10)⁻¹. Below the corner the line falls at 20 dB/decade from 20 log₁₀ K at ω = 1; above it at 40 dB/decade.

2. (a) Split ∫₀ᵗ = ∫₀^∞ − ∫_t^∞ in the convolution with e^{jω(t − τ)}. (c) |1 + j|, |1 + 0.1j| and arctan 0.1. (d) Minimise (1 − u²)² + 4ζ²u² over u² and evaluate at u = 1.

3. A notch has zeros exactly on the imaginary axis at ±jω_m, so its magnitude there is zero; its phase jumps by 180° through ω_m. Use a fine linear grid in Hz for (c).

4. The transient is a sum of decaying exponentials; the slowest one, e^{−1.068 t}, sets how long the fit must wait.

5. (b) A RHP zero adds lag where its mirror adds lead. (c) Think about what a small amplitude meets in a gear train and in a motor.

6. |e^{−jωT}| = 1. The Padé phase is −2 arctan(ωT/2).
