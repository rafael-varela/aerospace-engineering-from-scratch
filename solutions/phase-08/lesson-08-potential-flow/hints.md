# P08.08  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. The stagnation points are where the surface speed −2U sin θ − Γ/(2πa) vanishes; the suction peak is where its magnitude is largest.
2. (a) On |z| = a, a²/z is the complex conjugate of z. (b) Only the sin θ part of the pressure survives the integral of p n over the circle; the cos θ terms cancel by symmetry. (c) The residue theorem picks out the coefficient of 1/z in (dW/dz)².
3. On the body, ψ takes the value of the dividing streamline that comes from the stagnation point on the negative x axis, where θ = π. Far downstream the source's whole flux m passes between the body's two halves.
4. For the flat plate, c_l = 2π sin α; the equivalent cylinder's stagnation point lies at sin θ = −Γ/(4πUa). In the plate plane, the force is perpendicular to the free stream, not to the plate.
5. A weak source makes a thin body close to the two points ±a; a strong one makes a nearly circular body. Look at what the source–sink pair becomes as m → ∞ with the body size growing.
6. In a real fluid the circulation is set by viscosity near the surface and the flow separates; ask what fixes Γ in each case.
