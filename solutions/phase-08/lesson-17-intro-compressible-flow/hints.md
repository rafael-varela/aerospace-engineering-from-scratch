# P08.17  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Mach number is V/a with a = √(γRT) at the local static temperature; q_c is p(p₀/p − 1), not ½ρV².
2. (a) h = c_pT and c_p = γR/(γ − 1), so V²/(2c_pT) = (γ − 1)M²/2. (b) (1 + x)^n ≈ 1 + nx + n(n − 1)x²/2 + … with x = (γ − 1)M²/2 and n = γ/(γ − 1). (c) Eliminate the velocities with mass conservation and write the momentum balance in terms of M with ρu² = γpM².
3. Start from the stagnation chamber and go down to M = 0.8 with the isentropic ratios. The density along the streamline falls by 26 % between the chamber and the test section.
4. A normal shock stands in front of the probe; the probe then sees subsonic flow behind it and compresses that isentropically. The isentropic formula ignores the shock's total-pressure loss.
5. CAS uses the sea-level pressure and speed of sound, not the local ones; EAS uses the local density. At sea level all three coincide.
6. The density change grows like M²/2 and the airspeed error like M²/8: the same flow can be incompressible for one purpose and not for another.
