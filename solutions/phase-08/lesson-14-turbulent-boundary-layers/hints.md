# P08.14  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. u_τ = U√(c_f/2); the viscous length ν/u_τ is the unit of y⁺. The fuselage Reynolds number is above the range the 1/7th-law fit was made for: note it.
2. (a) Write y dU/dy from the inner form and from the outer form; one depends only on y⁺, the other only on y/δ, so both must be a constant. (b) ∫₀¹(1 − η^(1/7))dη and ∫₀¹η^(1/7)(1 − η^(1/7))dη; then separate variables in δ^(1/4)dδ ∝ dx. (c) Drag = turbulent drag over 0..L − turbulent drag over 0..x_tr + laminar drag over 0..x_tr.
3. A Clauser fit chooses the points it trusts; the answer depends on that choice. Find where the two log laws cross.
4. The momentum match starts the turbulent layer with the small laminar θ at transition; Prandtl's subtraction starts it as if it had grown from the leading edge.
5. Π sets the wake's share of the edge velocity: a larger Ue⁺ at the same δ⁺ means a smaller c_f = 2/Ue⁺².
6. Transition depends on surface condition and the air; the flat plate ignores thickness, pressure gradient and three-dimensional effects.
