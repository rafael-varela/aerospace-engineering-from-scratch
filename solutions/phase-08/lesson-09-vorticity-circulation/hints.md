# P08.09  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. The circulation within r is Γ(1 − e^{−r²/r_c²}); near the axis, 1 − e^{−x} ≈ x, which turns the swirl into u_θ ∝ r.
2. (a) With Euler, Du/Dt = −∇p/ρ − ∇Φ; a barotropic fluid has ∇p/ρ = ∇P(p) for some function P, and u·du = d(|u|²/2). (b) In polar form ∇²ω = ω_rr + ω_r/r; differentiate the Gaussian with r_c² a function of t. (c) Put the filament on the x axis, with the field point at height d, and let the end points go to ±∞ (or 0 and ∞).
3. A line vortex induces Γ/(2πd) at distance d, up on one side and down on the other. For the laminar decay, use r_c² = 4νt and set the circulation within 5 m to Γ₀/2.
4. The particles stay on their circles, so the loop's circulation by Stokes's theorem is the vorticity integrated over the initial disk at the current time. Halve the segment length and watch the drift.
5. Superpose the three filaments; the bound vortex's contribution at the tail is that of a finite segment seen from behind.
6. The point-vortex model gives w = Γ/(2πd) at the wing; divide by V and by α, with Γ = πcV sin α.
