# P08.05  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Sutherland's law gives μ at the ISA temperature; Re is based on the length that sets the boundary layer you care about.
2. (a) The dimensions of a product of powers are the matrix–vector product D e. (c) A function of Re and M that makes F independent of ρ must be proportional to 1/Re. (d) Two subspaces of the same dimension, one contained in the other, are equal.
3. ρ, n and d contain M, T and L between them and are dimensionally independent. Tip Mach number and advance ratio fixed means both the rotational speed and the flight speed scale with a/d.
4. Re/M = ρac/μ: at fixed fluid state, a smaller c needs a larger ρa/μ. Raising ρ (pressure) or lowering T (which lowers μ and a, but μ faster) both help.
5. Pick ρ, V, d, k as repeating: they span M, L, T and Θ. Any group can be multiplied by powers of the others; St = h/(ρVc_p) = Nu/(Re Pr).
6. Ask which physics dominates: viscosity (Re), compressibility (M), gravity waves (Froude, V/√(gL)), unsteadiness (reduced frequency), heat transfer (Pr, Nu).
