# P04.08  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. R = ρL/(πd²/4). The Thévenin resistance of a divider seen from its output is R₁ ∥ R₂.
2. (a) Each resistor stamps +g on two diagonals and −g on two off-diagonals; the source adds a row and a column. (b) No galvanometer current means V₂ = V₃: two dividers with equal ratios. (c) A resistor shared by two loops carries the difference of their currents. (d) The source moves charge CV_s through a potential difference V_s.
3. C = 4πε₀R; Q = CV; outside a charged sphere the field and potential are those of a point charge at its centre.
4. The formula sizes the wire at the nominal current. Resistive loads draw less current when the voltage drops; constant-power loads draw more.
5. Loading error is R_th/(R_m + R_th). The midpoint of two equal resistors sees R/2.
6. τ = RC; peak current V_s/R at t = 0; 99 % at τ ln 100. The half-energy rule does not depend on R.
7. Ask, for each, which element of the model (temperature-independent R, infinite meter resistance, ideal wires, constant-resistance loads, a reference node) is no longer true.
