# P04.07  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. All three speeds are √(c RT/M) with c = 2, 8/π, 3. n = p/kT. The kinetic energy needs no mass at all.
2. (a) Momentum per hit × hits per unit time, then sum. (b) The hemisphere average of cos θ weighted by the solid angle is 1/4 of the full sphere. (c) Substitute x = v√(m/2kT); the peak is where the derivative of v² e^{−av²} vanishes. (d) p/ρ = RT/M.
3. ρ = pM/(RT). γ = (f + 2)/f, so f = 2/(γ − 1). Use the escape speed in `fraction_above` with the gas's own molar mass.
4. h in metres for the ISA functions. q = ½ρV². For (d), compare Kn with 0.01 and remember where the ISA stops.
5. Collisions are pair collisions: n_collisions = (per-molecule count) × N, applied incrementally. The kurtosis of a finite sample scatters; compare the relaxed value with that scatter.
6. For each case ask: is the gas dense, hot, rarefied, or non-standard, and which of those breaks which formula?
