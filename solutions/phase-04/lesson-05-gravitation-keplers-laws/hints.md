# P04.05  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Everything follows from r = R + h and μ: v = √(μ/r), T = 2π√(r³/μ), ε = −μ/2r, v_esc = √(2μ/r). Escape from a circular orbit needs v_esc − v_c.
2. (a) At both apsides v ⊥ r, so h = r_p v_p = r_a v_a. Eliminate one speed. (b) Evaluate h at periapsis with v_p from (a). (c) The area swept in one period is the whole ellipse. (d) The cross product of a vector with itself is zero.
3. Areostationary is Kepler's third law with T = one sidereal day of Mars. For (c), compare the angular speed of Phobos with that of the surface.
4. `perigee_state` and `vis_viva` for (a). Energy error ∝ h², so the steps needed scale as √(error ratio). In (d), a positive period difference means the satellite arrives late.
5. a_t = (r₁ + r₂)/2; use vis-viva at both ends. A plane change Δv is 2v sin(Δi/2): small v makes it cheap.
6. Ask for each case: which force is missing, how large is its effect over the stated time, and is the required accuracy above or below it?
