# P00.11  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. First digit over 100, second digit over 10, last two over 100. Millimetres follow from multiplying a fraction by the chord in millimetres.
2. Three unknowns, three conditions; use $y_c'(p) = 0$ first to eliminate $b$, then $y_c(1) = 0$ to eliminate $c$. At $x = 0$ and $x = 1$ the slopes are the derivative expressions from the lesson evaluated at the ends.
3. L/D is the slope of a line from the origin to a point on the polar. Lay a ruler through the origin and rotate it until it just touches the curve; the touching point is the answer. The code does the same thing by computing $C_l/C_d$ at every point.
4. The slope hardly changes when all points shift by the same $u$ (a vertical shift changes the intercept, not the slope); the end-point removal and the scatter between points are what move it. Report the spread honestly even if it is small.
5. The linear region is governed by the pressure distribution over the whole section (barely viscous); the stall is governed by where the boundary layer separates (entirely viscous).
6. $Re = \rho V c / \mu$. Then ask which quantities in the lesson's §2.6 and §2.7 were said to depend strongly on Reynolds number.
7. A summary states findings in numbers a reader can use; an abstract describes what the document contains so the reader can decide whether to open it.
