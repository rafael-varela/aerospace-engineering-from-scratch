# P06.15  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. For a tube, A = π(D² − d²)/4 and I = π(D⁴ − d⁴)/64. The frequencies scale with √(EI/(ρAL⁴)).
2. (b) Two clamp conditions remove two constants; the two free-end conditions give a 2 × 2 determinant; use cosh² − sinh² = 1. (d) ∫φⱼφᵢ⁗ dx = [φⱼφᵢ‴ − φⱼ′φᵢ″] + ∫φⱼ″φᵢ″ dx. (e) Use sin(a + b) + sin(a − b) = 2 sin a cos b, twice.
3. A free end has no moment and no shear. A body that can translate and rotate freely has two zero-frequency modes.
4. Count elements per half-wavelength of the highest mode you need. A point mass lowers mode i in proportion to φᵢ² at the mass.
5. Euler–Bernoulli ignores shear strain and rotary inertia, which matter when the beam's depth is not small compared with the wavelength.
