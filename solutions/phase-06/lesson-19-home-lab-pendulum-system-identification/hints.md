# P06.19  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. f₀ goes as L^(−1/2) and g^(1/2); compare the two relative uncertainties before combining them. For a detectable difference compare the change in ω(A)/ω₀ with 7 × 10⁻⁵.
2. (a) Over one cycle ∫ sin²(ωt) dt = π/ω, ∫|sin ωt|³ dt = 8/(3ω) and ∫|sin ωt| dt = 4/ω. (c) With u = 1/A the equation becomes linear in u.
3. The model is right this time (a linear mode with viscous damping): what should the residual RMS be?
4. A covariance shrinks as 1/√N only if the residuals are independent. What happens to the residuals when the model error grows with the record length?
5. A mixture has no single exponent; p is whatever compromise fits. The amplitude range decides whether a slope and an offset in ζ_eq(A) can be told apart.
6. A bias that is the same in every repeat cannot show up in the scatter of repeats. In short windows the frequency and ζ barely change, so the damped cosine is nearly right there.
