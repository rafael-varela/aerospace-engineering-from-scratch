# P06.04  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. Ω in ENU is Ω(0, cos λ, sin λ); the fictitious Coriolis acceleration is −2Ω × v. The equivalent bank angle is the angle whose tangent is the sideways acceleration over g.
2. (b) Differentiate v = v_O + v_rel + ω × r with the transport theorem; one ω × v_rel comes from differentiating v_rel, the other from differentiating ω × r. (e) Work in the non-turning axes first, where the Coriolis term is gone.
3. Released "at rest" in the station, the spanner has the inertial velocity of the point where it was: ωr tangentially, smaller than the floor's ωR. In the inertial frame it flies a straight tangent line.
4. All the calls are in the lesson's `main`; change the latitude and azimuth. Cross-range is positive to the right of the line of fire.
5. The flat formula's cross-range for a northward shot is the east component of `first_order_impact_shift`. For (b), think about what closes the loop for the airliner (it measures where it is) and what does not for the inertial navigator.
