# P06.13  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. I = bt³/12 with t the thickness in the bending direction; the root stress at resonance scales with the base acceleration and with 1/(2ζ).
2. (a) Kinetic energy ½∫ρA ẇ² dx with ẇ = ẇ_tip × shape; integrate the square of the shape. (b) Write everything in complex amplitudes and divide. (c) At r = 1 the steady part and the homogeneous part have the same frequency (to first order in ζ). (d) In each half cycle the friction is a constant force: the motion is a shifted cosine.
3. Both sensors read the same relative transmissibility; one uses the region r ≪ 1, the other r ≫ 1. Damping near 0.6–0.7 flattens the gain and makes the phase nearly linear in frequency.
4. Signal-to-noise falls as e^{−ζω_n t}: count the cycles before the peak is ten times the noise. The stress is inversely proportional to ζ.
5. In each half cycle with viscous damping, the decay factor is √q and the friction shifts the centre by ±d.
6. A notch scales the input by (allowed response)/(predicted response). Friction, microslip and stress concentrations all depend on level and on geometry, not on the SDOF model.
