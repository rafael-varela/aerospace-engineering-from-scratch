# P06.05  Hints

One hint per problem. A hint points at the idea, not the answer. Read `method.md` next if the hint is not enough.

1. For Grashof, sort the four lengths and compare s + l with p + q; then see which link is the shortest. In (c), ω × r in the plane is ω(−r_y, r_x).
2. (a) Expand sin θ₃ cos θ₄ − cos θ₃ sin θ₄. (c) Suppose I_jl is not on the line through I_jk and I_kl and compare the directions of its two velocities. (d) In a parallelogram the coupler stays parallel to the ground.
3. At an extreme of the rocker, B is at distance r₃ + r₂ (extended) or r₃ − r₂ (folded) from O₂, and r₄ from O₄: a triangle with three known sides.
4. Change one number in the lesson's `main` (or copy its sweep); for (c), build the crank history θ₂(t) with the given ω₂ and α₂ centred on the evaluation time.
5. Torque needed is M₄ × (ω₄/ω₂) at its largest; free play at the surface is the servo backlash times the gearing.
