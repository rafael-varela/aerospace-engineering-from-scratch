# P02.19  Problem set

Attempt every problem before opening `solutions/`. Record what you tried. State units on every numerical answer and say which results are exact, which are numerical, and which are statistical estimates with their scatter.

Several problems use your personal seed **s**: the day of the month you were born (1–31). Every record in this set is synthetic, generated by your own `generate_gust` or `white_noise`; say so wherever you report one.

## 1. (Warm-up: Dryden numbers by hand)

A glider flies at V = 50 m/s through turbulence with σ_w = 1 + s/20 m/s and L_w = 200 + 10s m.

(a) Compute the break frequency b = V/L in rad/s and in Hz, and Φ_w(0). (b) Show by differentiating (5) that Φ_w has its maximum at x = 1/√3, and give the frequency and the value of the maximum as a multiple of Φ_w(0). (c) Give the integral time scale and the lag at which R_w first crosses zero. (d) Confirm (a)–(c) with `dryden_psd` and `dryden_autocorrelation`.

Answer form: five numbers with units, one short derivation, one code check.

## 2. (Derivation: normalisation, transform pair and scatter)

(a) Prove ∫₀^∞ Φ_w dω = σ_w² from (5), evaluating the two x-integrals with the substitution x = tan θ.
(b) Starting from R_w(τ) in (6), compute its two-sided transform S_w(ω) and show that S_w/π is (5). (Use the transforms of e^{−a|τ|} and |τ|e^{−a|τ|}.)
(c) Show that ∫₀^∞ R_w² dτ = 5σ_w⁴L/(16V) and hence derive the relative standard deviation √(5L/(4VT)) of the variance estimate, stating the assumption on T.
(d) Show that ∫₀^∞ ω²Φ_w dω diverges and explain in one sentence what that means for the gust velocity's time derivative.

Answer form: four short derivations.

## 3. (Unfamiliar scenario: gyro noise integrates to an angle random walk)

A rate gyro's output noise is white, with one-sided PSD G₀ = (0.02 + s/1000)² (deg/s)²/Hz. The attitude estimator integrates the rate for t = 600 s with no other information.

(a) Treat the integrator as the filter H(iω) = 1/(iω). Write the output spectrum and show that its variance over all frequencies diverges, yet the variance of θ(t) = ∫₀^t n dt′ at a fixed t is finite: Var θ(t) = S₀t with S₀ the two-sided PSD. Express S₀ in terms of G₀.
(b) Compute σ_θ(600 s) in degrees.
(c) Simulate it: draw `white_noise(n, dt, rng)` with dt = 0.01 s, scale by √S₀, integrate with a cumulative sum times dt, and record θ(600 s) for 200 seeds. Compare the SD across seeds with (b) and state the scatter you expect in an SD estimated from 200 samples.
(d) Run `welch_psd` on one 600 s noise record and verify that its level between 1 and 40 Hz is G₀.

Answer form: a derivation, two numbers with units and scatter, one code check.

## 4. (Numerical, uses your code: a heavier aircraft)

A utility aircraft of mass 5000 + 100s kg, S = 30 m², C_Lα = 5.5 rad⁻¹, flies at V = 100 m/s at 300 m (ρ = 1.19 kg/m³) through the lesson turbulence (σ_w = 1.5 m/s, L_w = 300 m).

(a) Compute τ, σ_v, σ_a and the RMS load factor σ_n with `heave_time_constant` and `heave_response_variances`; compare σ_n with the sharp-edged-gust value σ_w/(gτ).
(b) Generate a 2000 s record at Δt = 0.02 s with seed s, simulate the heave response, and report the sample σ_w, σ_v and σ_n with the scatter you expect from (13).
(c) Compute the fraction of time |Δn| > 0.25 g from the Gaussian model and from the record.
(d) Compute the Welch spectrum of Δn (8192-point segments), overlay |H_a|²Φ_w/g², and report the band ratios in the three decades 0.01–0.1, 0.1–1 and 1–10 Hz. At what frequency does the load-factor spectrum peak, and why is it near √(bc)/(2π)?

Answer form: a table of RMS values with scatter, two fractions, three band ratios, one frequency with an explanation.

## 5. (Judgment: what the model can and cannot say)

(a) Of the quantities computed in this lesson (σ_w, σ_v, σ_a, the crossing rate of v, the exceedance fraction at 0.5 g), which depend strongly on the ω⁻² tail of the Dryden spectrum and which do not? Give the reasoning, not a list.
(b) Real low-altitude turbulence over mixed terrain is not stationary. Name one statistic of a flight record that would reveal it, and say how long a record you could still trust for σ_w.
(c) Why does Rice's formula fail for the load factor of this aircraft model, and what physics would have to be added before it could be applied?
(d) A ride-comfort criterion limits the RMS vertical load factor to 0.10 g. With σ_n = 0.135 g at 60 m/s in the lesson turbulence, decide what airspeed meets the criterion (use the scaling of σ_a² with V for r ≫ 1), and state which assumption of the model you would check before trusting that number to better than 10 %.

Answer form: four short paragraphs, each with a number or a named quantity.

## 6. (Hard, uses your code: designing a flight-test record)

A flight test must estimate σ_w² to 10 % (relative SD) and resolve the gust spectrum with a frequency resolution of 0.005 Hz, in the lesson turbulence (L = 300 m, V = 60 m/s), sampled at 50 Hz.

(a) From (13), find the record length T that gives 10 %.
(b) For the resolution, what segment length (in seconds and in samples) does Welch's method need? How many 50 %-overlapping segments K fit in T, and what relative scatter (≈ 1/√K) does each spectral bin then carry?
(c) Generate the record with seed s, compute the Welch estimate with that segment length, and report: the variance ratio to σ_w², the ratio of the estimate to the Dryden model at 0.01 Hz, the band average over 0.01–0.1 Hz, and the per-bin SD/mean of the ratio between 0.05 and 0.5 Hz. Compare with (b).
(d) Explain why the two requirements conflict, and propose (with numbers) what you would report to the customer: a variance with its error, and a spectrum with its resolution and its per-bin error, or a smoothed spectrum with a coarser resolution.

Answer form: two record lengths, a segment count, four numerical results with expected scatter, one recommendation with numbers.
