## What Phase 06 must achieve

Engineering dynamics and vibrations at the level of a second-year dynamics course and a third-year vibrations course (MIT 2.003 and 16.07, Meriam, Hibbeler, Thomson or Inman scope), taught through aircraft and spacecraft. The organising idea of the phase is *kinematics, kinetics, and an independent check*: every lesson states the frame it works in, writes the kinematics before the forces, and checks its main result by an independent route. The admissible checks are a closed form against `scipy.integrate.solve_ivp`; conservation of energy or of angular momentum to a stated tolerance; Lagrange against Newton–Euler for the same system; DCM against quaternion; rotating-frame against inertial-frame integration; modal superposition against direct integration; a finite-difference or finite-element eigenproblem against the closed-form frequency equation; analytic Grms against a time-domain simulation; and, in lesson 19, a model against the learner's own measurement. A learner who finishes can describe particle motion in Cartesian, polar, cylindrical and path coordinates; compute maneuver load factors; use work–energy and impulse–momentum for impacts and arrested landings; work in rotating frames including the Earth's; analyse planar linkages and rolling and sliding bodies; represent and propagate 3-D attitude with Euler angles, DCMs and quaternions; integrate Euler's equations and explain spin stability; derive equations of motion by Lagrange and Hamilton; compute the free, forced, modal, continuous and random response of vibrating structures; design a vibration isolator; build a small multibody model; and identify the frequency and damping of a real pendulum with uncertainties — with a tested Python module for each.

## Test harness (mandatory)

Tests import the implementation through the repo's `conftest.py`:

```python
from conftest import load_impl
impl = load_impl(__file__)
```

`pytest curriculum/phase-06/lesson-NN-<slug>` runs against the learner's `starter/main.py`; `AEFS_IMPL=solution pytest curriculum/phase-06/lesson-NN-<slug>` runs against `solutions/phase-06/lesson-NN-<slug>/solution.py`. **Your solution.py must pass your tests**; run the second command from the repo root before you finish. Also run the first command: every failure must be a `NotImplementedError` raised by the starter (a test that passes against the starter is acceptable only if it tests nothing the learner writes, such as a pure helper already given). Run `python3 scripts/check_lessons.py` (structure linter; it must report no ERROR for your lessons).

The starter has the same function signatures and docstrings with `raise NotImplementedError` bodies (helpers that are not the point of the lesson may be given complete), plus a working `main()` that produces the lesson's output once the functions are filled in. Tests assert properties, not one magic number: conservation residuals, orthonormality (CᵀC = I, det C = +1, |q| = 1), symmetry, limiting cases (zero damping, zero spin, infinite stiffness, small angle), agreement between two methods, a textbook closed form, convergence order, and dimensional sanity. **12–30 tests per lesson. Each lesson's tests run in under 10 s** (time them; use short spans and loose-but-justified tolerances for `solve_ivp`, and `rtol`/`atol` stated explicitly). No network access in tests. No files written outside `tmp_path`. Anything random is seeded (`numpy.random.default_rng(seed)`) with statistical tolerances justified in a comment.

Every lesson has an independent check that its main result must reproduce, chosen from the list in "What Phase 06 must achieve", and the lesson's part 5 shows it. Use NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and SymPy only; no dynamics, multibody, rotation or vibration libraries in tested code (`scipy.spatial.transform.Rotation` may appear in a test or in part 3's "Compare" step as an independent check of lessons 07 and 08, never inside the learner's implementation).

## Physical experiment (lesson 19)

- Lesson 19 owns `projects/p06-pendulum-sysid/` with `bom.yaml` (three tiers: `minimum_cost`, `recommended`, `enhanced`, as in the earlier kits; prices as ranges in USD with `price_checked` dated; `reuse_from: [p04-pendulum]`, plus `p00-calibration` if the kitchen scale is reused), `safety.md` (the full safety box in the format of `projects/p04-pendulum/safety.md`), `procedure.md`, `report-template.md` and `data/README.md`.
- Apparatus: the `p04-pendulum` string pendulum, reused. Minimum-cost tier: phone video against a protractor or grid, angle read frame by frame (or with any free video-tracking tool the learner prefers, named neutrally). Recommended: the phone's own gyroscope logged by a free sensor-logging app with the phone *as the bob* in a padded pouch (total ≤ 0.2 kg) or a phone video with a high-contrast marker. Enhanced: a small IMU breakout on the `p04-electronics-bench` microcontroller, logged over USB. The prediction (frequency from L and g, the large-amplitude correction, and an order-of-magnitude damping ratio, all with uncertainties) is written down and dated before the first swing.
- Analysis: extract the angle (or angular rate) time series; estimate the damped frequency (zero crossings and FFT peak), the damping ratio by logarithmic decrement over n cycles, and both together by nonlinear least squares on θ(t) = A e^(−ζωₙt) cos(ω_d t + φ) + c with `scipy.optimize.curve_fit`, reporting parameter uncertainties from the covariance and checking them against a seeded bootstrap or the scatter of repeat runs. Discuss amplitude-dependent frequency (the P04.06 correction), and whether the decay is exponential (viscous) or linear in time (Coulomb pivot friction) or amplitude-dependent (quadratic air drag), with a test that distinguishes them.
- Hard limits stated in `safety.md` and repeated in the lesson: string length ≤ 2 m; bob mass ≤ 0.2 kg; release amplitude ≤ 45°; the swing arc clear of people, pets, screens and glass; no powered drive of the pendulum.
- Synthetic data: the solution provides `make_synthetic_swing(seed, ...)` that generates a seeded synthetic record (nonlinear pendulum integrated with `solve_ivp`, light viscous plus quadratic damping, sampling jitter and measurement noise). A committed copy lives at `projects/p06-pendulum-sysid/data/SYNTHETIC_seed<seed>_swing.csv`, generated by the solution (not by the tests) and reproducible from the seed. It is labelled **SYNTHETIC** in its file name, its header comment, and every table caption and plot title derived from it. A learner without the kit completes the lesson with it; the report says so.

## Content standards

- Follow the six-part arc and the metadata table from `LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` exactly, including `**Time** | unmeasured`.
- Audience: a motivated adult who has finished Phases 00–05. Plain language, short sentences, define each term on first use. SI units (angles in radians in code, degrees allowed in prose with the unit stated).
- Every lesson states a concrete *engineering question* in the first paragraph, derives in part 2, builds in part 3 (with **Verify** and **Compare** steps), runs an **experiment** in part 4 (physical in lesson 19; otherwise a computational experiment such as a parameter sweep, a sensitivity study, a method comparison, or a comparison with an authentic public dataset whose source and license are named), validates in part 5 by the independent route, and ends part 6 with **adversarial self-checks** and 5–8 **check-yourself** questions (answers in `worked-solutions.md`).
- Aerospace examples throughout: a fighter pull-up and a sustained turn, carrier arrested landing, a long-range projectile and wind on a rotating Earth, control-surface linkages, a landing-gear wheel spinning up at touchdown, aircraft and spacecraft attitude (3-2-1 yaw-pitch-roll, star-tracker quaternions), the Explorer 1 flat spin, a spinning upper stage, a double pendulum as a slung load, a satellite's Kepler orbit in phase space, a wing's bending modes, launch random vibration (a NASA-STD-7001-style input spectrum), an IMU isolator, a landing-gear drop test. Numbers must be realistic and labelled *typical* or *illustrative* where they are not from a cited source.
- **Every number in `lesson.md` and `worked-solutions.md` must be reproduced by `solution.py` or by a probe script you run.** Keep probe scripts in the scratchpad under a unique name (`p06_lNN_probe.py`); do not commit them. If you change code, re-run the probes and update the prose.
- References: cite only sources in `references/sources.yaml`, by `@id`, with exact section titles from `references/phase-06.md` (being written in parallel; read it before you write any citation and re-read it before you finalise). Where the map says GAP or partial GAP, write original instruction and say nothing about missing references; cite only ids that exist in `references/sources.yaml` at the time you write. Never cite or require a paid resource; paid books may be named only under an "Optional textbooks for deeper study" note. Never paste text from a source; write your own explanation and link.
- Problem sets: 5–7 problems, graded easy → hard, at least one derivation or proof, one unfamiliar scenario, one that uses the lesson's code, one judgment question. Prefer parameterized problems (a seed or the learner's own numbers).
- Solutions: `hints.md` (one hint per problem), `method.md` (steps, no final numbers), `worked-solutions.md` (assumptions, units, derivation, result, independent check, common mistakes, plus check-yourself answers).
- Code: Python 3.11+, NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib/SymPy only; type hints; docstrings; `if __name__ == "__main__": main()`. Matplotlib must use the `Agg` backend (`matplotlib.use("Agg")` before importing pyplot) in code that runs under tests; `main()` saves figures to a path argument and closes every figure (`plt.close(fig)`) rather than calling `plt.show()`. Any test that calls a plotting or file-writing function passes `tmp_path`.
- Honesty rules: never claim the course grants a degree or credential or is equivalent to one; never present simulation as physical measurement; label synthetic data as synthetic everywhere it appears; say what a model assumes and where it fails. No invented time estimates.
- Safety: no at-home combustion, motor firing, pressure vessels, high-speed unguarded rotors or autonomous outdoor flight, as a requirement or an extension.
- Length: `lesson.md` roughly 250–500 lines. Depth over padding.
