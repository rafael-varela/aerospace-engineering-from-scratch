# Phase 06 Grading rubric

Used for the Phase 06 mini-project and for grading the exam's Part D. **The mini-project is the pendulum system-identification report from P06.19** (Home Lab: Pendulum System Identification, `projects/p06-pendulum-sysid`): the frequency and damping of the `p04-pendulum` string pendulum identified from the learner's own swing record, with uncertainties, and compared with a prediction written down and dated before the first swing. It is the only physical experiment of the phase, and it exercises the whole of what the phase is about, which is kinematics, kinetics and an independent check: the learner states the frame and the generalised coordinate (lesson 01 and lesson 11), derives the equation of motion of a nonlinear pendulum with damping by Newton or Lagrange (lessons 02, 06 and 11), predicts its natural frequency from the length to the centre of mass (with the physical-pendulum correction for a finite bob) and the large-amplitude correction of P04.06, with an uncertainty budget (`P00.14`), estimates the damped frequency by zero crossings and by an FFT peak and the damping ratio by logarithmic decrement (lesson 13), fits both together by nonlinear least squares with `scipy.optimize.curve_fit` and checks the covariance uncertainties against a seeded bootstrap or the scatter of repeat releases, and must decide whether the decay is viscous, Coulomb (pivot friction) or quadratic (air drag) with a test that distinguishes them. A comparison of the frequency against the prediction and of the fitted damping law against a drag estimate is the independent check; the usual discrepancy is a length defined to the wrong point of the bob, a forgotten amplitude correction, or a damping ratio quoted from a single window as if it were a constant, rather than a fault in the dynamics. A second release amplitude separates amplitude-dependent frequency and damping from a constant error, so a good report contains a real model test, not only a fitted ζ. A learner without a kit completes the mini-project with the seeded synthetic dataset supplied by the lesson (`make_synthetic_swing`, committed as `projects/p06-pendulum-sysid/data/SYNTHETIC_seed<seed>_swing.csv`), labels it synthetic everywhere it appears, and is graded on the same rows except that the experiment-design row grades the *design* (prediction, procedure, uncertainty budget) and the report must say that no physical measurement was made; such a report is recorded as *practice completed*, never as a measured result.

**Hard requirements for the pendulum report** (and for the exam's Part D question 24 when answered with the learner's own data):

1. **Safety box completed.** The report contains the project's safety box (`projects/p06-pendulum-sysid/safety.md`, in the format of `projects/p04-pendulum/safety.md`: hazards, energy limits, controls, supervision, location, regulations, stop criteria, disposal, not permitted), filled in for the setup actually used, with any deviation from the procedure stated. **A report without a completed safety box is not graded**, whatever else it contains. The box must record: string length ≤ 2 m; bob mass ≤ 0.2 kg (a phone used as the bob only in a padded pouch with the total within 0.2 kg); release amplitude ≤ 45°; the swing arc clear of people, pets, screens and glass; and no powered drive of the pendulum.
2. **Prediction dated before measurement.** `data/prediction.md` (or the equivalent) holds the predicted small-amplitude period or frequency, from the measured length (stating to which point of the bob it is measured) and the local g, the amplitude-corrected period at the planned release, and an order-of-magnitude damping ratio, each with its uncertainty, and the date; that date precedes the first raw-data file. A prediction written or revised after the data were seen is reported as a post-hoc correction and graded as no prediction (row 3 at most *developing*).
3. **Uncertainty reported.** Every measured and every predicted number carries a standard uncertainty with its type (A, B or combined) and the coverage factor if expanded; every comparison is a normalised error, not "close". Fit uncertainties from a covariance matrix are reported together with a bootstrap, a repeat-release or a window-to-window check, and the report says which is larger. A number without an uncertainty is graded *insufficient* in row 2.
4. **Measurement, computation and synthetic data kept apart.** Raw records (video frame times and angles, or logged gyroscope samples) are kept unedited, including every release; every value computed by a model, a fit or a simulation (the predicted period, the fitted ζ and ω_d, a `solve_ivp` reconstruction) is labelled as computed; synthetic data are labelled **SYNTHETIC** in every table, figure and caption. Presenting a simulation or a synthetic dataset as a measurement fails row 5 outright.

Weights are proposed and will be calibrated in pilots. **Minimum thresholds apply in the dynamics, experiment and safety rows: presentation quality never compensates for wrong dynamics, an undated prediction, a missing uncertainty or an unsafe method.**

## Grading Part D of the exam

Questions 24 and 25 are graded on the same rows, scaled to a question: row 2 (the period fit with both uncertainty types and a correct E_n, and the decrement fit; in 25, every number recomputed with the right units and formula), row 4 (ranked causes using the amplitude dependence, the damping law separated from its alternatives, and the window fits read as a model error rather than a precise measurement; in 25, the isolator error found and the correct item left alone), and row 5 (honest conclusions, measured versus predicted versus computed versus synthetic kept apart, and the hard requirements and safety limits named). A learner who answers question 24 with their own `p06-pendulum-sysid` data attaches the dated prediction file and the completed safety box; without them the answer is graded as if the synthetic table had been used, and the own-data claim is not recorded.

## Passing Phase 06

Pass requires: exam Parts A–D each at proficient level or above for the competencies they tag, **and** the mini-project at or above the minimum in every row with a weighted score of 3.0 or more. Report the outcome per competency as *practice completed*, *self-assessed mastery*, or *externally reviewed mastery* (the last only when a peer, mentor, or club reviewer has graded the report against this rubric). A mini-project completed with synthetic data only is recorded as *practice completed* for competency 4 until a measured report is graded. None of these is a credential, and nothing in this phase certifies anyone to analyse, test or sign off real aircraft or spacecraft dynamics, vibration or loads.
