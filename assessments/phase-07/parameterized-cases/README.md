# Phase 07 parameterized validation cases

Unseen inputs for the computational problems, so answers cannot be copied. Each case is a JSON file `{"lesson": "P07.NN", "seed": int, "inputs": {...}, "expected": {...}, "tolerance": {...}}`. Generate new ones with `generate.py` (which uses the reference solutions) and check a learner's implementation with `run.py`. Expected values come from a closed form wherever the thermodynamics has one, and otherwise from an independent oracle: a conservation law evaluated by separate code (an energy, entropy, exergy, element or dry-air/water balance that must close to round-off), `scipy.integrate.quad` or `solve_ivp` on the governing equation written independently, `scipy.optimize.brentq` on the defining equation, the law of mass action from ΔG° for equilibrium, and a second solver (`scipy.optimize.minimize` for Gibbs minimisation, a closed-form series for conduction) where the lesson has one. Property data are the phase's and only the phase's: the NASA Glenn 9-coefficient polynomials (`@nasa-tp-2002-211556-glenn-coefficients`, from `@nasa-cea-github`), the Yan/NIST SRD 69 water and ammonia rows (`@libretexts-yan-engineering-thermodynamics`), and Lienhard Tables A.1, A.3, A.6, 10.1 and 10.5 (`@lienhard-heat-transfer-textbook`); seeds draw states only inside the ranges those data cover (200–6000 K for the NASA fits, the embedded table rows, the table temperature ranges), so that no case extrapolates a property. Every case also contains a *limit* or a *balance residual* that its physics must reproduce exactly (an ideal component, zero heat transfer, a vanishing temperature difference, a constant-cp gas, an isothermal enclosure, an infinite or vanishing Biot number), so an implementation that returns the right number at the generated point by tuning cannot pass. Tolerances are set so that a correct from-scratch implementation passes and a specific, named physics or convention error fails; each bullet states both, with the size of the error taken from a probe run on the exam's own numbers. Anything random inside a case (states, geometry, loads, noise added to a synthetic record, Monte Carlo samples) is generated from the case's seed with `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)`, and statistical tolerances are stated with the sample count that justifies them. No case contains a measurement; inputs that imitate a measurement (a cooling record, a table with a planted error) are labelled `"synthetic": true` in the JSON, and `run.py` prints that label with the result. Combustion and equilibrium cases are computational only: there is no at-home combustion, flame or fuel of any kind in this phase.

Cases planned, one family per computational lesson:

- `p07-01-ideal-gas-*.json` (P07.01): a species from the phase's NASA set or a seeded ideal-gas mixture (2–6 species with seeded mole fractions), and 3–8 states from the seed (T in 200–6000 K, p in 1 kPa–10 MPa) given by a seeded independent pair from (p, T, v, h, u, s), plus a seeded heating interval → cp, h, s and γ at each state, the completed state, Δh with variable cp and with a constant cp, and the exit temperature of a given heat addition. **Oracle:** `quad` of the cp polynomial for Δh and of cp/T for Δs°, written separately from the h and s° forms; `brentq` on h(T) and s(T, p) for the inversions. **Limit:** h(298.15 K) equals the tabulated enthalpy of formation within 3 J/mol (the database's R = 8.314510 against CODATA 2018, 2.24 J/mol for CO₂); He gives cp = 5/2 R exactly; a one-species mixture reduces to the species; dependent pairs ((T, h), (T, u), (h, u)) must raise an error, not return a state. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁸ on every property and 10⁻⁸ K on inversions, so a wrong sign on the a₁ or a₂ term, a mixture entropy missing its mixing term (162.8 J/(kg·K) for dry air), temperatures entered in °C, and the textbook constant cp used in place of the fit (10.5 % low on the exam's 600–1250 K heating) all fail.
- `p07-02-blowdown-*.json` (P07.02): a rigid tank of N₂ or He with seeded V, p₀ (1–40 MPa), T₀, orifice diameter and Cd, back pressure and stop pressure, and a heat mode (adiabatic, isothermal, or a seeded wall conductance hA) → m₀, ṁ₀, τ, the unchoking pressure, the time, temperature and mass at the stop pressure, the isothermal heat, the recovery pressure, and the energy residual of the learner's integration. **Oracle:** the closed forms (adiabatic p/p₀ = (1 + (γ − 1)t/(2τ))^(−2γ/(γ−1)), isothermal e^(−t/τ)) while choked; for finite hA, `solve_ivp` (DOP853, rtol 10⁻¹¹) on the tank ODE written independently. **Limit:** hA = 0 reproduces the adiabatic closed form and hA → ∞ the isothermal one; the isothermal heat is RT₀(m₀ − m) exactly; U + ∫ṁh dt − ∫Q̇ dt = U₀. **Tolerance:** closed-form outputs rtol 10⁻⁷ and the energy residual below 10⁻¹² of U₀, so a missing Γ in the choked flow (τ too short by a factor 1.728), the leaving gas carrying u instead of h (which removes the cooling and returns the isothermal 305.2 s instead of the adiabatic 258.2 s in the exam's tank), and the remaining-mass fraction taken as p/p₀ in an adiabatic blowdown (0.100 instead of 0.193) all fail.
- `p07-03-tables-*.json` (P07.03): water or ammonia, a seeded set of (T, v), (T, p) and (p, T) states inside the embedded rows, a seeded rigid-tank fill and soak, and, for the table-check family, a copy of the embedded saturation table with one entry corrupted by a seeded amount (`"synthetic": true`) → phase, quality, p, u, h, s, the fill limit, the heat absorbed, and the index of the corrupted entry. **Oracle:** the lever rule and linear interpolation written independently; the corrupted entry found by the h = u + pv residual against its round-off bound and by the Clausius–Clapeyron residual against its neighbours. **Limit:** at a table node interpolation returns the node exactly; x = 0 and 1 return the saturated values; at low pressure Z → 1. **Tolerance:** node values exact to 10⁻¹², interpolated values rtol 10⁻⁹ against the oracle, the corrupted index exactly; so p_sat interpolated linearly in T instead of ln p against 1/T (up to 5.7 % between water rows, 0.96 % at the exam's 23 °C cabin), a chord dp/dT in the Clausius–Clapeyron check (+0.13 % at 150 °C, against +0.0003 % by the central log difference), and a fill limit computed from v_g instead of v_f all fail; a corruption of 2 kJ/kg in any one h must be found (twice the largest round-off bound in the tables, about 1 kJ/kg for saturated vapour at 0.01 °C).
- `p07-04-entropy-*.json` (P07.04): a seeded ledger of 3–8 processes drawn from heat across a finite ΔT, two bodies equilibrating, throttling, duct friction, a two-stream heat exchanger, stream mixing, gas mixing and liquid-line friction, with seeded states, and 1–4 seeded cycle claims → each S_gen, the total, the lost work at a seeded T₀, the ranking, and accept/reject for each claim. **Oracle:** the entropy balances in closed form, the exchanger and mixer outlet temperatures by `brentq` on the energy balance with the NASA dry-air h; for each cycle, −ΣQ_k/T_k. **Limit:** ΔT → 0 gives S_gen → 0 with slopes 1 (fixed Q) and 2 (two bodies) on a seeded sequence; equal-temperature mixing generates nothing; a Carnot cycle has a zero Clausius integral. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰ on every S_gen and the accept/reject verdict exactly, so a throttle written as R ln(p₂/p₁) (negative), heat-transfer entropy with temperatures in °C, a constant-cp exchanger in place of the NASA one (2.3 % low on Ṡ_gen and 4.4 % low on the duty for the exam's precooler) and a cycle test that accepts any η below 1 all fail.
- `p07-05-compressor-*.json` (P07.05): T₁, an overall pressure ratio (1.2–60), a polytropic or isentropic efficiency, a stage count, a machine type (compressor or turbine) and a gas (NASA air or a seeded constant-cp gas) → T₂ₛ, T₂, η_s, η_p, w, s_gen, and the multistage exit temperature. **Oracle:** `brentq` on s°(T₂ₛ) − s°(T₁) = R ln π and on the polytropic integral; `quad` of cp dT for the work; for constant cp the closed forms (π^m − 1)/(π^(m/η_p) − 1) and R ln π(1/η_p − 1). **Limit:** η = 1 gives s_gen = 0 to 10⁻⁹; a constant-cp gas reproduces T₁π^((γ−1)/γ) to 10⁻¹¹; many stages converge to the polytropic exit temperature at first order. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁹ on temperatures and work, so a polytropic efficiency used as an isentropic one (752.6 K instead of 778.3 K at the exam's π = 25), constant cp used for NASA air (+22.3 K and +0.94 % work there), and a turbine efficiency defined as the compressor's inverse all fail.
- `p07-06-brayton-*.json` (P07.06): T₁, p₁, a pressure ratio, a turbine inlet temperature (900–2000 K) and a gas → the state table, w_c, w_t, w_net, q_in, q_out, η, the back-work ratio, the Carnot efficiency, the work-optimal pressure ratio and the enclosed areas from the learner's contour integral on a stated grid. **Oracle:** for constant cp the closed forms η = 1 − π^(−m), w_net = cpT₁[τ(1 − 1/x) − (x − 1)] and π_opt = τ^(γ/(2(γ−1))); for NASA air the optimum from `minimize_scalar` and the condition T₂ = T₄. **Limit:** w_net = 0 at π = 1 and at π_max, where η equals Carnot; the isentropic legs add nothing to ∮T ds. **Tolerance:** state and performance rtol 10⁻⁹, the optimum rtol 10⁻⁵, the contour integrals within 10⁻⁵ relative at n = 400 with an observed order 2 ± 0.2; so the closed-form optimum used for NASA air (17.94 instead of 22.37 at the exam's 1500 K), the efficiency evaluated with temperatures in °C (0.947 instead of 0.482 in the exam's check sheet), and a T–s area that includes the isentropes' non-zero contribution all fail.
- `p07-07-real-cycle-*.json` (P07.07): a seeded `CycleSpec` (π 2–40, TIT, η_c, η_t, pressure losses, an intercooler with or without a set exit temperature and split, a recuperator effectiveness) and a gas → the station table, every component's energy residual and s_gen, the total s_gen against the Clausius integral, η, w_net, and the best intercooler split. **Oracle:** for constant cp with ideal components the closed forms 1 − (T₁/T₃)π^m and 1 − 2(T₁/T₃)(x^½ − 1)/(1 − 1/x); the Gouy–Stodola identity w_net = q_in(1 − T₀/T_H) − T₀Σs_gen as an independent closure of energy and entropy bookkeeping. **Limit:** ε = 0, no intercooler and ideal components recover P07.06 to 10⁻¹⁰; the best split is √π for constant cp without loss; η < Carnot always. **Tolerance:** component residuals below 10⁻³ J/kg, the Gouy–Stodola closure below 10⁻⁹ relative, η rtol 10⁻⁸; so a recuperator effectiveness referred to T₂ instead of the turbine exit, a recuperator kept above the crossover (η 0.3796 instead of 0.3889 at the exam's π = 25 with the lesson's losses), and turbine expansion that ignores the downstream pressure losses all fail.
- `p07-08-exergy-*.json` (P07.08): a seeded `EngineSpec` (T₀, p₀, V₀, π_d, π_c, η_c, TIT, Δ_cc, η_t, η_n) → the station states, f, every component's T₀S_gen and its exergy-balance destruction, the fuel exergy, the jet product, the exhaust exergy, the audit residual, η_II and the component second-law efficiencies. **Oracle:** the two independent routes to each destruction (entropy and exergy balance) and the audit identity; the fuel exergy against −ΔG° of the reaction (within 3 %). **Limit:** the reversible limit (π_d = η_c = η_t = η_n = 1, Δ_cc = 0) gives zero turbomachine destruction and component η_II = 1 exactly; flow exergy vanishes at the dead state and equals RT₀ ln(p/p₀) at T₀. **Tolerance:** the audit closes to 10⁻¹² of the input and the two routes agree to 10⁻⁹ J/kg; η_II rtol 10⁻⁸; so a fuel exergy taken as the LHV (η_II 0.382 instead of 0.367 at the lesson's design point), product mixtures without the mixing term, and an exergy of heat taken at T₀ instead of the boundary temperature all fail.
- `p07-09-psychrometrics-*.json` (P07.09): seeded moist-air states (T 0.01–60 °C at the table's range, p 20–110 kPa, ω or φ), 2–3 streams to mix, a cooling process with or without condensation, and an ECS case (occupants, fresh air per occupant, moisture per occupant, recirculation fraction, cabin altitude, load) → ω, p_v, φ, the dew point with its estimate flag, the mixed state, the condensate and duty, and the cabin and supply states with the three residuals. **Oracle:** the dry-air, water and energy balances written independently; p_sat from the table rows and, independently, the NASA Gibbs-equality estimate (within 1 % from 0.01 to 60 °C). **Limit:** mixing a stream with itself changes nothing; the cabin humidity does not depend on the recirculation fraction; cooling above the dew point condenses nothing. **Tolerance:** balances to 10⁻¹² kg/s and 10⁻⁶ W, states rtol 10⁻⁹; so ω computed with p in place of p − p_v (0.63 % low at the exam's cabin), recirculation counted in the cabin water balance, a sub-zero dew point not flagged as an estimate, and the latent heat of the condensate left out of the pack duty all fail.
- `p07-10-combustion-*.json` (P07.10, computational only): a fuel (CH₄, H₂, Jet-A(g)), an oxidizer (air or O₂), a seeded equivalence ratio (0.3–1.5), reactant temperatures, and a target turbine inlet temperature → reactants and products, f and f_st, LHV and HHV, the complete-combustion T_ad, and the φ for the target. **Oracle:** atom balances checked element by element; Hess's law on the tabulated enthalpies of formation; `brentq` on H_products(T) = H_reactants written separately. **Limit:** φ → 0 returns the inlet temperature; T_ad peaks at φ = 1 for complete combustion; HHV − LHV equals the water formed times h_fg from the steam table within 0.1 %. **Tolerance:** atoms to 10⁻¹³, heating values rtol 10⁻⁸, T_ad within 10⁻⁶ K; so the HHV used where the LHV belongs, the air's argon and CO₂ dropped from the reactants, and a constant product cp from 298 K (+326 K on the exam's CH₄/air at φ = 0.8) all fail.
- `p07-11-equilibrium-*.json` (P07.11, computational only): seeded element amounts (H/O or C/H/O/N/Ar from a fuel–oxidizer pair at a seeded φ), T (1000–4000 K) and p (0.01–200 bar), and a flame case → the equilibrium mole fractions, the element potentials, G/RT, the mass-action residuals, and the equilibrium flame temperature. **Oracle:** `scipy.optimize.minimize` (SLSQP) on G/RT with the element constraints, which must reach the same G to 10⁻⁹ relative and never lower; ln Q = ln K from ΔG° for every independent formation reaction. **Limit:** at 1000 K the composition is complete combustion to 10⁻⁶; elements conserved to 10⁻¹²; the equilibrium T_ad lies below the complete-combustion value for lean and stoichiometric mixtures. **Tolerance:** major-species mole fractions atol 10⁻⁶, G/RT rtol 10⁻⁹, ln Q − ln K below 10⁻⁶; so a chemical potential missing the ln(p/p°) term (which returns the 1-bar composition at every pressure: x_H₂O 0.639 instead of 0.827 at 3000 K and 10 bar) and a one-reaction solver that omits OH, H and O (0.897 there) both fail.
- `p07-12-conduction-*.json` (P07.12): a seeded layered wall with convective faces, a seeded fin, a 2-D rectangle with seeded edge conditions and generation, and a 1-D transient (material from Table A.1, thickness, h, gas and limit temperatures, θ, grid) → interface temperatures and flux, the fin heat rate, the learner's 2-D field at seeded points and its global energy balance, the transient field and its energy budget, and the time to the surface limit. **Oracle:** series resistances; the fin closed form; the Fourier series of the rectangle; erf and Lienhard eq. 5.53 for thick walls; the slab series for thin ones. **Limit:** the global steady balance and the transient energy budget close to round-off on any grid; Bi → 0 reproduces the lumped time and √(αt) ≪ L the semi-infinite one; explicit marching above Fo = ½ must be detected. **Tolerance:** closed forms rtol 10⁻¹⁰, the balances below 10⁻⁹ of the heat flow, the numerical fields within 2 × 10⁻³ of the oracle at the stated grid with an observed order 2 ± 0.2; so the semi-infinite time used for a 20 mm copper wall (9.78 s instead of 5.20 s in the exam), the fixed node's half cell left out of a stored-energy comparison with the semi-infinite formula (4.8 % short at n = 80 in the exam's Part C), and a one-sided boundary difference (first order) all fail.
- `p07-13-convection-*.json` (P07.13): a seeded geometry (flat plate, cylinder in cross-flow, pipe, vertical plate, horizontal cylinder or plate), fluid (air or water), wall and fluid temperatures and speed, inside the tables' temperature range, plus a seeded box or cold-plate application → the film properties, Re or Ra, Nu, h, the heat rate, and the application's surface temperature, required speed or wall temperature, with the correlation used and its range. **Oracle:** the correlations written independently; `quad` of the local plate law for the mixed average; the first law on the coolant. **Limit:** average = 2 × local at the trailing edge (laminar); St Pr^(2/3) = c_f/2 exactly (laminar); Churchill–Bernstein → 0.3 as Re → 0; out-of-range inputs must raise an error, not return a number. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁹ on Nu and h, and the range verdict exactly; so properties taken at the free-stream instead of the film temperature, Gnielinski used at Re ≈ 2000 (a 47.3 °C wall instead of 65.8 °C in the exam's check sheet), and the plate's local law used as its average all fail.
- `p07-14-radiation-*.json` (P07.14): a seeded pair of rectangles (parallel or sharing an edge), a 2-D configuration for crossed strings, a seeded 3–6 surface enclosure with mixed T and Q conditions, and a seeded radiator case (Q, T, ε, α_s, sun angle, planetary IR) → view factors (closed form and the learner's numerical integral on a stated grid), radiosities, net heats and unknown temperatures, and the radiator area or temperature. **Oracle:** the closed forms; reciprocity and summation; the two-surface and reradiating-wall network formulas; the radiator energy balance. **Limit:** an isothermal enclosure gives zero net heat for any emittances; ε = 1 gives the black-body exchange; ΣQ_i = 0 to round-off. **Tolerance:** closed forms rtol 10⁻¹⁰, enclosures rtol 10⁻¹⁰, numerical view factors within 3 × 10⁻³ at n = 40 with orders 2 ± 0.2 (parallel) and 1 ± 0.2 (shared edge); so an eclipse area used for a sunlit radiator (0.825 instead of 1.882 m² in the exam), the linearised coefficient taken as 4εσT_sur³ (18 % low for a 60 °C body in a 20 °C room), and reciprocity applied with the areas swapped all fail.
- `p07-15-cooling-*.json` (P07.15): a seeded SYNTHETIC cooling record (`"synthetic": true`) from the lesson's generator with seeded truth (convection factor, ε, dimensions, room temperature, initial temperature, probe lag, noise) → the predicted τ and its budget, the log-linear and nonlinear τ with uncertainties and χ², the windowed τ, the lagged-fit τ_p, h and E_n, the Biot number, and the variable-h factor. **Oracle:** the generating truth (the record is made from `solve_ivp` of the variable-h model, so its windowed τ are known); `curve_fit` with `absolute_sigma=True` on the planted exponential for the fit's statistics. **Limit:** a planted single exponential with Gaussian noise is recovered with pull standard deviation ≈ 1 and χ²/dof ≈ 1 (500 seeded replicates; tolerance ± 0.13 on the pull standard deviation, four standard errors of a sample standard deviation, 1/√(2(N − 1)) = 0.032, at that count); the short-cylinder series reduces to the lumped exponential as Bi → 0. **Tolerance:** τ within 3 u(τ) of the truth after the probe-lag cut, h rtol 10⁻⁶ from τ, the Biot number rtol 10⁻¹⁰; so a fit with T∞ left free (τ 16 % short on the exam's seed-715 record), ln T fitted instead of ln θ, and the probe-lag transient left in the log-linear fit all fail.

These are written once the reference solutions for lessons 01 to 15 are merged; `generate.py` then produces them deterministically from a seed. Every Phase 07 lesson is computational except lesson 15, whose physical report the rubric grades; its case family above tests only the reduction code on SYNTHETIC records, since its unseen input is the learner's own bar.
