# Phase 02 Grading rubric

Used for the Phase 02 mini-project and for grading the exam's Part D. **The mini-project is the thrust-curve fit with confidence bands from P02.18** (Regression & Curve Fitting): a data-based investigation that fits a thrust model to a published static-fire thrust curve with a least-squares solver written from scratch, reports the fitted parameters and the total impulse with confidence and prediction bands, tests the residuals, and checks the fit against limiting cases and against `scipy.optimize.curve_fit` or `numpy.linalg.lstsq`. It is chosen over the P02.19 Dryden gust generator because it exercises more of the phase (least squares from P01.17, the normal and t distributions from lesson 16, hypothesis tests on the residuals from lesson 17, the integral of the fitted curve as a first-order ODE check from lesson 01, and a Bayesian or bootstrap view of the parameter uncertainty from lesson 20) and because it works on authentic measurements rather than on numbers generated by the learner's own code. The Dryden generator is a strong second choice and may be substituted by a learner who documents a validated spectrum (lessons 10 and 19) against the analytic form, but its data are synthetic by construction, so the provenance requirement below then applies to the *spectrum model* (which Dryden form, which parameters, from which published source) rather than to a data set. Phase 02 has no physical experiment, so the Phase 00 row on experiment design is replaced by a row on data handling, and the safety hard requirement is replaced by a **data-provenance** hard requirement: the report must say where the thrust curve came from (manufacturer, certification body or test campaign; the document or file and its date), under what conditions it was taken (propellant temperature, sample rate, whether the published curve is averaged over several firings or a single firing, any smoothing applied by the publisher), and that the learner did not measure it; any synthetic data used alongside it must be labelled synthetic in every figure, table and sentence where it appears. Weights are proposed and will be calibrated in pilots. **Minimum thresholds apply in the mathematics rows: presentation quality never compensates for a wrong derivation or for a result that the code does not reproduce.**

| Dimension | Weight | 4 (exemplary) | 3 (proficient) | 2 (developing) | 1 (insufficient) | Minimum |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correct mathematics, derivation, assumptions | 25% | Normal equations (or the linearised Gauss–Newton step for a nonlinear thrust model) derived from the least-squares condition; the model stated with its assumptions (which burn phases it covers, independent Gaussian residuals of constant variance, no correlation between consecutive samples) and the time range where it holds; the confidence band derived from the parameter covariance s²(AᵀA)⁻¹ and the prediction band from it plus s², with the t quantile and its degrees of freedom stated; total impulse derived as the integral of the fitted curve with its propagated uncertainty, not read off a plot | Derivation correct; model, residual assumptions and range of validity stated; bands derived, not quoted | A step asserted rather than derived, or the band formula used without stating the degrees of freedom or the residual assumptions | Wrong normal equations or covariance, confidence and prediction bands confused, or the model fitted through ignition or tail-off transients without comment | 3 |
| Calculation and numerical correctness | 20% | Solver written from scratch (normal equations with elimination, QR, or Gauss–Newton) and checked against `lstsq` or `curve_fit`; every number in the report reproduced by the submitted code; residuals orthogonal to the design columns to round-off; the total impulse checked by two independent quadratures (trapezoid on the data and the closed-form integral of the fit); significant figures match the uncertainty; any random step (bootstrap, Monte Carlo) seeded | Correct results, code runs and agrees with the library | Results plausible but code does not reproduce them, or disagrees with the library without explanation | Arithmetic, unit (N versus N·s, ms versus s) or indexing errors in the fit or the impulse | 2 |
| Data handling and model selection | 20% | Provenance box complete; raw data kept unchanged and the reduction scripted; ignition and tail-off samples excluded with a stated criterion; a competing model (one more term, a different burn-profile shape, or a piecewise fit) fitted and accepted or rejected on evidence (an F-test or an information criterion from lesson 17, or the residual pattern); residuals tested for normality and for autocorrelation and the consequences for the bands stated | Provenance stated; data reduced reproducibly; fit range justified; one residual check done | Data partly transcribed by hand, or the fit range chosen by eye without a criterion, or residuals not examined | No provenance, or data edited to improve the fit, or synthetic data presented as measured | 2 |
| Verification, validation, sensitivity | 20% | Fit checked against limiting cases (zero thrust before ignition and after burnout, total impulse within the publisher's stated value, average thrust = impulse/burn time); the band checked by a bootstrap or Monte Carlo over the residuals and compared with the analytic band; a leave-one-out or perturbed-sample-rate test done and interpreted; the sensitivity of the impulse to the chosen fit range reported | Limiting cases checked; one of bootstrap, leave-one-out or range sensitivity done and interpreted | Fit shown but not validated against anything outside itself | Agreement claimed without a check, or "the fit looks good" | 2 |
| Communication, reproducibility, honesty about limits | 15% | Anyone could reproduce the fit from the report and repository; figures captioned with units, the data source and the band's confidence level; the report says what the model cannot predict (another motor, another propellant temperature, a single firing's scatter beyond the prediction band) and never presents a fitted curve as a measurement or a band as a guarantee | Clear report, code and data included, limits stated | Report incomplete or hard to follow; limits implicit | Missing code or data, a fitted curve presented as measured, or synthetic data not labelled | Provenance box present (hard requirement) |

## Passing Phase 02

Pass requires: exam Parts A–D each at proficient level or above for the competencies they tag, **and** the mini-project at or above the minimum in every row with a weighted score of 3.0 or more. Report the outcome per competency as *practice completed*, *self-assessed mastery*, or *externally reviewed mastery* (the last only when a peer, mentor, or club reviewer has graded the report against this rubric). None of these is a credential.
