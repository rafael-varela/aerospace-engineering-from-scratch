# Phase 10 parameterized validation cases

Unseen inputs for the computational problems, so that answers cannot be copied. Each case is a JSON file `{"lesson": "P10.NN", "seed": int, "inputs": {...}, "expected": {...}, "tolerance": {...}}`. Generate new ones with `generate.py`, which uses the reference solutions, and check a learner's implementation with `run.py`.

**Expected values.** They come from a closed form wherever the aerodynamics has one, and otherwise from an independent oracle, a second model one level away in the phase's hierarchy:

- the exact Joukowski or Karman–Trefftz flow for the panel method;
- thin-airfoil theory as the thickness goes to zero;
- the elliptic closed form CL = a₀α/(1 + a₀/(πAR)), e = 1 for lifting line;
- lifting line for an unswept high-aspect-ratio lattice;
- actuator-disk momentum theory for BEM;
- the quasi-steady limit and a direct numerical Fourier transform of the indicial function for Theodorsen and Wagner;
- `scipy.integrate.solve_bvp` (Falkner–Skan) for Thwaites;
- `scipy.integrate.quad` for every integral with a smooth integrand;
- `scipy.special.hankel2` for C(k).

**Limits and residuals.** Every case also contains a *limit* or a *residual* that its model must reproduce exactly, so an implementation that returns the right number at the generated point by tuning cannot pass. Examples: a symmetric section at α = 0 gives cl = 0; a flow-tangency or Kutta residual; a conservation law (momentum and energy in the actuator disk); symmetry of a span load; linearity in α; thickness → 0, AR → ∞, k → 0 or h/b → ∞; an observed order of convergence.

**Tolerances.** They are set so that a correct from-scratch implementation passes and a specific, named error fails. Each bullet states both, with the size of the error taken from a probe run on the exam's own numbers.

**Random inputs and synthetic data.** Anything random inside a case (geometry, angles, noise added to a synthetic balance or bench record) is generated from the case's seed with `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)`, and statistical tolerances are stated with the sample count that justifies them. No case contains a measurement. Inputs that imitate a measurement (a balance record, a bench sweep) are labelled `"synthetic": true` in the JSON, and `run.py` prints that label with the result. Cases that compare with Ladson's NACA 0012 data or the NACA finite-wing slopes read them from `curriculum/phase-10/data/` and carry `"authentic": true` with the report and table, never a copy of the values.

Cases planned, one family per computational lesson:

- `p10-01-balance-*.json` (P10.01): a seeded synthetic balance record (`"synthetic": true`) for a section model: chord, span, speed, balance-centre offsets in x and z, 10–30 angles, noise on N, A, M, α and q → cl, cd and c_m about a seeded reference point, the aerodynamic centre and c_m,ac, and first-order uncertainties. **Oracle:** the generator's noise-free truth; the a.c. from four reference points by separate code; Monte Carlo for the uncertainties. **Limit:** noise-free readings reduce to the truth to 10⁻¹²; the a.c. is the same from every reference point; a pure couple is unchanged by the transfer; √(N² + A²) = √(L² + D²). **Tolerance:** noise-free outputs rtol 10⁻¹⁰. Skipping the rotation fails, since it turns the exam's cd = +0.00908 into −0.0788. So does forgetting the vertical balance offset, which biases c_m by −0.0047 there. Linear uncertainties must match a 4000-sample Monte Carlo within 5 %.
- `p10-02-naca-*.json` (P10.02): a seeded 4-digit or standard 5-digit designation, a point count, open or closed trailing edge, a chord → coordinates in Selig order, area, maximum thickness and its position, maximum camber and its position, leading-edge radius, the 5-digit constants r and k₁. **Oracle:** `quad` of the thickness polynomial for the area; `brentq` on r³ − 3r² + 6pr − 3p² = 0; thin-airfoil quadrature split at r for k₁. **Limit:** a symmetric section is symmetric to 10⁻¹⁵; the closed trailing edge has zero thickness; the polygon area converges at order 2.0 ± 0.1 on cosine spacing. **Tolerance:** coordinates atol 10⁻¹² c. Adding the thickness vertically instead of along the camber-line normal fails: it moves the upper surface by up to 0.0031c on a 2412 and 0.0095c on a 4415 at the same x. Uniform spacing fails the order test (1.51 in the exam's question 6).
- `p10-03-kutta-*.json` (P10.03): a seeded Joukowski or Karman–Trefftz circle (b, μ, trailing-edge angle), α and a contour shape (circle, ellipse, rectangle) → Γ, cl, the zero-lift angle, the lift slope, and the lift by surface pressure, Blasius and momentum. **Oracle:** the closed form Γ = 4πVa sin(α + β); `quad` round the contour. **Limit:** a contour that does not enclose the body gives Γ = 0 to 10⁻¹²; the pressure drag is zero to the quadrature error; τ = 0 reproduces Joukowski. **Tolerance:** contour integrals rtol 10⁻⁹, surface pressure rtol 10⁻⁶ with 1024 points. Taking α_L0 = +β fails: for the exam's section it gives cl = 0.0968 at 4° instead of 0.8448.
- `p10-04-thin-airfoil-*.json` (P10.04): a seeded 4-digit camber line, a parabolic or 5-digit line, a plain flap (E, δ) and α → A₀, A₁, A₂, cl, α_L0, c_m,c/4, x_cp, τ and dc_m/dδ. **Oracle:** closed forms for the parabola and the flap; adaptive `quad` split at the kinks for the 4-digit line; a discrete-vortex solution as a second route. **Limit:** a flat plate gives cl = 2πα exactly with one lumped vortex; the lift slope is 2π for every camber line; a flap with E → 1 gives τ → 1. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰. A hinge placed at cos θ_h = 1 − 2E fails (τ = 0.9423 instead of 0.6090 for E = 0.25). An unsplit 64-point Gauss rule across the 2412's kink leaves an α_L0 error of 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ rad and fails a 10⁻⁸ tolerance on that integral.
- `p10-05-panel-*.json` (P10.05): a seeded Karman–Trefftz section (b, μ, trailing-edge angle), α and a panel count, or a seeded NACA 4-digit section → node γ, cl from Γ and from Cp, c_m,c/4, Cp at the midpoints. **Oracle:** the exact conformal-map solution (cl, c_m, Cp at each midpoint's circle angle). **Limit:** the tangency and Kutta residuals below 10⁻¹⁰; a symmetric section at α = 0 gives cl = 0; the solution at any α is cos α times the α = 0 one plus sin α times the α = 90° one. **Tolerance:** the cl error falls at an observed order of 1.6–2.4 over three doublings (the exam's question 9 shows 1.74–2.06), and below 10⁻³ relative by N = 160. The midpoint Cp error on 5–95 % of the chord falls monotonically. A self-influence taken on the inside of the surface (β = +π) fails the residual and the order test together.
- `p10-06-boundary-layer-*.json` (P10.06): a seeded edge-velocity distribution (a power law, Howarth's retarded flow, or a panel solution of a seeded NACA section at a seeded Re) → θ, H, λ, the laminar-separation and transition stations, the turbulent θ and H at the trailing edge, and cd by Squire–Young. **Oracle:** Falkner–Skan by `solve_bvp` for the similarity cases; θ² = 0.45νs^{1−m}/(K(5m + 1)) for power laws; the 1/7-power-law plate for Head's method. **Limit:** a stagnation point gives θ² = 0.075ν/C; a flat plate gives √0.45 for Thwaites; a symmetric section at α = 0 gives equal upper and lower drag. **Tolerance:** Thwaites rtol 10⁻⁶ against the closed forms. Applying 2θ/c without the Squire–Young factor fails (20.8 % high on the exam's question 10 states). So does the trapezoid rule on ∫u_e⁵ds next to a stagnation point, which is three times too large in the first interval.
- `p10-07-inverse-*.json` (P10.07): a seeded target load (uniform, roof-top with a seeded break, or a seeded sine series) → the Glauert coefficients, α_i, the camber line at seeded stations; and a seeded decision matrix (3–7 candidates, 2–5 criteria, weights) → totals, the winner and the exact flip weights. **Oracle:** the a = 1.0 closed form z = −(cl_i/4π)[x ln x + (1 − x) ln(1 − x)]; the forward thin-airfoil analysis by cosine projection; brute-force weight scans for the flip points. **Limit:** a load symmetric about mid-chord gives α_i = 0; z(1) = 0 to 10⁻¹²; doubling the load doubles the camber; a dominated candidate never wins. **Tolerance:** camber atol 2 × 10⁻⁶ c with 200 terms, and an observed truncation order of 2 ± 0.2. Flip weights must match to 10⁻¹⁰, so a numerical search with a coarse step fails. So does normalising a smaller-is-better criterion upward: with the suction peak scored the wrong way, the exam's question 11 totals become 0.700, 0.324 and 0.300 and the 0012 replaces the 4415 as the winner.
- `p10-08-high-lift-*.json` (P10.08): a seeded flap chord ratio, deflection and base camber; a seeded mass, area, approach speed and Δcl_max → θ_h, τ, Δcl, Δα_L0, Δc_m, the hinge-moment derivatives, CL_max by the stated 3-D rule, V_s and the approach margin. **Oracle:** the closed forms; the thin-airfoil quadrature of the kinked camber line. **Limit:** the largest |Δc_m| per radian is 3√3/8 at E = 0.25; τ → (4/π)√E for small E; τ → 1 as E → 1. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰. Using the section cl_max as the wing CL_max fails: the exam's question 26 gets V_s = 25.0 m/s instead of 26.4 m/s.
- `p10-09-horseshoe-*.json` (P10.09): seeded vortex segments and field points; a seeded span load (elliptic, or with sin 3θ and sin 5θ terms) on a seeded strip layout → induced velocities, near-field and Trefftz-plane induced drag, e; a seeded C_D0, e and weight → the polar optima. **Oracle:** the symmetric-segment closed form (Γ/2πh)L/√(L² + h²); Glauert's e = 1/(1 + Σn(Aₙ/A₁)²); the closed-form optima. **Limit:** circulation ±Γ round each trailing leg and zero round the whole wake; the near-field drag equals the Trefftz drag; e ≤ 1 for every planar load. **Tolerance:** velocities rtol 10⁻¹². The elliptic CDi must converge at observed order 2 ± 0.1. A span efficiency above 1 fails outright, so a layout with control points at the strip mid-spans is caught.
- `p10-10-lifting-line-*.json` (P10.10): a seeded straight wing (span, area, taper, linear twist, a₀ and α_L0, possibly anti-symmetric twist) and α → the coefficients Aₙ, CL, CDi, e, the rolling moment, the span load, the lift slope and τ. **Oracle:** the elliptic closed form; quadrature of Γ and Γα_i along the span; a horseshoe-stack solution from P10.09 for two-term loads. **Limit:** the elliptic wing has every higher coefficient below 10⁻¹²; a symmetric wing has no even coefficients; anti-symmetric twist leaves CL unchanged. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁸ at N = 80. The observed order must be 4 ± 0.3 for an untapered wing and 2 ± 0.3 for a tapered one (the exam's question 24). Collocating at the tips gives a singular matrix and fails; so does summing δ from n = 1.
- `p10-11-vlm-*.json` (P10.11): a seeded trapezoidal planform (AR, taper, quarter-chord sweep, dihedral) and a lattice (nx, ny, spacing) → CL_α, CDi and e in the Trefftz plane, the spanwise load, Cm_α and the aerodynamic centre. **Oracle:** lifting line for unswept wings of AR ≥ 8; the single-panel closed form of the exam's question 15; Richardson extrapolation over three lattices. **Limit:** a symmetric planform gives a symmetric load to 10⁻¹²; the 2-D limit CL_α → 2π as AR → ∞; a half-wing with its image equals the full wing. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁹ against the reference lattice at the same nx and ny. With Hough spacing, CL must converge at observed order 2 ± 0.3. A control point at mid-panel instead of the three-quarter point fails: one panel gives CL_α = 2.959 instead of 5.574 per radian on the trainer's wing, and π instead of 2π in the 2-D limit.
- `p10-12-planform-*.json` (P10.12): a seeded AR, taper, sweep, twist distribution and design CL → e, the local cl/CL and the stall-onset station, the optimum twist (unconstrained, linear, constrained by a root-bending-moment ratio or a tip stall margin) and its e. **Oracle:** the Lagrange closed form of the linear-twist optimum; the elliptic load for the unconstrained optimum; a brute-force scan of the tip twist. **Limit:** unconstrained optimisation gives e → 1 as the twist stations multiply; an untwisted rectangle stalls at the root; Prandtl's bell gives span × √(3/2) and drag × 8/9. **Tolerance:** e rtol 10⁻⁶. The tip twist of the linear optimum must match the closed form to 10⁻⁴ degree (−2.614° for the trainer). A washout entered as positive twist moves the stall station outboard instead of inboard and fails.
- `p10-13-drag-build-up-*.json` (P10.13): seeded component geometry (chords, thicknesses, wetted areas or bodies of revolution), speed, transition locations, add-on drag areas and an Oswald model → C_f, FF, ΔC_D per component, C_D0, e, the polar optimum and the cruise drag. **Oracle:** the closed-form friction laws; `quad` of the body surface ∫2πr√(1 + r′²)dx; a brute-force L/D search. **Limit:** x_tr → 0 and → 1 recover the pure turbulent and laminar laws; C_D = 2C_D0 at the optimum; FF → 1 as t/c → 0. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰. Counting a wing's wetted area once instead of twice fails: ΔC_D falls from 0.00705 to 0.00353 and C_D0 from 0.0318 to 0.0281 in the exam's question 16. A case with `"authentic": true` reads Ladson's Table I and VII blocks and checks that the fully turbulent estimate lies above every free-transition point and below every grit point.
- `p10-14-low-re-*.json` (P10.14): a seeded section and cl, a seeded Reynolds number and bubble constant → the laminar-separation station, u_e there, the transition station, whether a bubble forms, its length, whether it closes, ΔCp_rec and the minimum Re for closure. **Oracle:** Thwaites' closed forms for power laws; the identity ℓ = N_ℓν/u_e,s. **Limit:** the separation station is identical at every Re to round-off; ℓ scales exactly as 1/Re; closure switches between 0.95 and 1.05 Re_min. **Tolerance:** separation station atol 10⁻⁴ c; Re_min rtol 10⁻⁶ (the exam's question 17 gives 5.51 × 10⁴ for the 0012 and 1.83 × 10⁵ for the 4402 at cl = 0.445). An implementation whose separation point moves with Re fails the invariance check at once.
- `p10-15-stall-*.json` (P10.15): seeded Kirchhoff parameters (a₀, α₁, S) with a synthetic noisy lift curve (`"synthetic": true`), or Ladson's Table I block at a seeded Re (`"authentic": true`); a seeded planform and twist → the fitted parameters with standard errors, the full-range section curve, the nonlinear lifting-line CL at seeded angles below stall, the first local stall station and its CL. **Oracle:** the generating parameters for synthetic curves; the linear lifting line for a straight-line section; the Fourier fixed point at a relaxation below its computed limit. **Limit:** f = 1 gives a₀α; f = 0 gives a₀α/4; the flat plate gives cl = sin 2α and cd = 2 sin²α; the nonlinear solver equals the linear one for a straight section. **Tolerance:** fitted parameters within four standard errors of the generator (the coverage to be confirmed on seeded replicates when `generate.py` is written); nonlinear CL rtol 10⁻⁸ against the fixed point where both converge. The fixed point must fail above its relaxation limit (0.062 for the trainer's rectangle), so a "solver" that always reports convergence fails.
- `p10-16-slender-body-*.json` (P10.16): a seeded body of revolution (spheroid, paraboloid nose, or a nose–cabin–tail profile), q and α; a seeded wing–tail layout → the loading, N, M about seeded points, C_Nα, Cm_α, Lamb's k₁ and k₂, the exact spheroid moment and the neutral-point shift. **Oracle:** the closed forms N = 2qαS_base and M = 2qα(Vol − S_base(L − x_ref)); Lamb's coefficients; `quad` of the loading. **Limit:** a closed body gives N = 0 and the same couple about every point; a paraboloid gives C_Nα = 2 with the centre of pressure at L/2; k₁ = k₂ = ½ for a sphere. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁶ on the integrals. A Munk moment with the wrong sign moves the neutral point aft instead of forward and fails. So does omitting k₂ − k₁ when the case asks for the exact spheroid (13 % at the trainer's fineness).
- `p10-17-bem-*.json` (P10.17): a seeded blade (radius, hub, chord and pitch laws, blade count, a section model), V and n → the BEM thrust, torque, power, C_T, C_P, η, the induced velocities and F along the blade; and a seeded thrust, V and area → the actuator-disk results. **Oracle:** momentum theory; a uniform-inflow blade designed by the case, which in the ideal limit must reproduce the disk exactly. **Limit:** C_P = 2πC_Q; momentum and energy conserved in the disk; the ideal uniform-inflow blade has η = η_i to 10⁻⁶; no blade beats η_i at its own C_T. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁶ against the reference BEM with the same elements. The factor-2 slip T = ρA(V + v)v fails: it gives v = 1.443 m/s and η_i = 0.893 instead of 0.760 m/s and 0.940 for the exam's UAV.
- `p10-18-rotor-*.json` (P10.18): a seeded rotor (mass, radius, blades, chord, tip speed, twist, a, C_d0, κ, f, K) → hover momentum and blade-element results, the collective, the figure of merit, Glauert's inflow at seeded μ, the power curve, the best speeds and the autorotation estimate. **Oracle:** the closed forms; Newton's method on Glauert's quartic checked against its two limits; disk integration of the profile power. **Limit:** blade-element induced power equals C_T^{3/2}/√2 with uniform inflow; λ_i → λ_h as μ → 0 and → C_T/(2μ) at high μ; the energy balance C_Tλ = C_Pi + C_Pp holds to 10⁻¹⁰ with κ = 1. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁸. Using K = 3 where the case states 4.65 fails at high speed: the exam's helicopter needs 188.13 kW instead of 192.71 kW at 60 m/s.
- `p10-19-unsteady-*.json` (P10.19): seeded reduced frequencies, pivots and motions (pitch, plunge, a step, a sharp-edged or 1 − cos gust) → C(k), the lift and moment amplitudes and phases, the work per cycle, the Wagner and Küssner responses and the Duhamel lift. **Oracle:** `scipy.special.hankel2` for C(k); the closed-form convolution of a sine with Jones's exponentials; a direct numerical transform of φ′. **Limit:** C(0) = 1 and C(∞) = ½; the lift tends to 2πα₃/₄ as k → 0; the circulatory moment vanishes about c/4; pure plunge is damped at every k. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰ on C(k), and the Duhamel quadrature at observed order 2 ± 0.1. A reduced frequency built on the chord instead of the semi-chord fails: the exam's 5 Hz case gives |cl|/α̂ = 5.073 at +48.3° instead of 4.465 at +18.0°. So does a conjugated C(k) (the e^{−iωt} convention), which gives +45.5°.
- `p10-20-ground-effect-*.json` (P10.20): a seeded rectangular or tapered lattice, height h/b, α and tail position → CL and CDi with and without the ground, K/K_free, the ground-plane residual, the downwash gradient at the tail. **Oracle:** the single-horseshoe closed form (4h/b_v)²/(1 + (4h/b_v)²); the 3-D Biot–Savart sum far downstream against the Trefftz formula. **Limit:** the ground-plane normal velocity below 10⁻¹² of the wing's own; h → ∞ recovers free air to 10⁻⁵; K/K_free falls monotonically as h falls. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁹ against the reference lattice; the empirical fit 1 − (1 − 1.32h/b)/(1.05 + 7.4h/b) within 0.05 over its stated range. An image with the same sign fails at once: the single-horseshoe ratio at h/b = 0.1 becomes 1.794 instead of 0.206.
- `p10-21-polar-*.json` (P10.21): a seeded set of labelled and deliberately unlabelled polar files (`"synthetic": true`, written into the case's temporary folder) and a seeded polar → the reader's accept or refuse verdicts, the parsed settings, cl_α, α_L0 and cd_min over a stated range, the RMS difference between two polars. **Oracle:** the generating headers and lines. **Limit:** a polar compared with itself gives zero; a polar shifted by Δα gives that shift in α_L0. **Tolerance:** verdicts exact. A reader that accepts a file without `version`, or an XFOIL polar without `ncrit`, fails (the two refusals of the exam's question 23). Fitted values rtol 10⁻¹⁰.
- `p10-22-vv-*.json` (P10.22): a seeded planform from the authentic finite-wing file (`"authentic": true`) or a seeded manufactured solution f* + Chᵖ, a seeded lattice triplet, κ and u_κ → the observed order, the Richardson value, the GCI, u_num, u_input, u_D, u_val and the verdict. **Oracle:** the manufactured solution, which the three-grid formulas must recover exactly; the stated half-widths of the data. **Limit:** a manufactured solution gives its own p and f* to 10⁻¹²; a symmetric planform gives a symmetric load. **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰ on the formulas. Treating a half-width as a standard uncertainty fails: the exam's TN-1669 row gets u_D = 0.00213 instead of 0.00123 per degree, a factor √3. So does a GCI reported with an unclipped observed order outside 0.5–2.

These cases are written once the reference solutions for lessons 01–22 are merged; `generate.py` then produces them deterministically from a seed. The bench-rig reduction of lesson 23 (the airspeed by two methods, the arm-ratio calibration, the tares, CL and CD with both uncertainty types and the blockage band, the slope and the comparison with Ladson's data) is covered by the exam's question 25 and graded through the report against the rubric. It needs no seeded case, since its unseen input is the learner's own rig. Lesson 21's tool polars are never generated by running XFOIL, AVL or OpenVSP: its cases test the reader, the parser and the metrics on synthetic files only.
