# Phase 00 Grading rubric

Used for the Phase 00 mini-project (the free-fall experiment report from P00.15, which also serves as the phase's integrated project) and for grading the exam's Part D. Weights are proposed and will be calibrated in pilots. **Minimum thresholds apply in the physics and safety rows: presentation quality never compensates for wrong physics or unsafe method.**

| Dimension | Weight | 4 (exemplary) | 3 (proficient) | 2 (developing) | 1 (insufficient) | Minimum |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correct physics, derivation, assumptions | 25% | Free-fall model stated with its assumptions (from rest, constant g, no drag); drag neglect justified quantitatively via terminal speed; propagation derived correctly | Model and assumptions correct; propagation correct | Minor errors in propagation or an assumption left implicit | Wrong formula, or drag/initial velocity ignored without comment | 3 |
| Calculation and numerical correctness | 20% | All reductions reproducible from raw data by the submitted code; g estimated from the t² vs h fit with uncertainty; significant figures correct | Correct results, code runs | Results plausible but code does not reproduce them, or sig figs wrong | Arithmetic or unit errors | 2 |
| Experiment design, data quality, uncertainty | 20% | Prediction recorded before measurement; ≥ 8 drops per height, 4 heights; both Type A and Type B terms; frame numbers recorded, not just differences; all data kept | Prediction first; adequate repeats; uncertainty estimated | Few repeats or no Type B term, or raw data partly discarded | No prediction before measurement, or no uncertainty | 2 |
| Verification, validation, discrepancy investigation | 20% | Normalized errors computed; disagreements ranked by cause; one follow-up test done and interpreted | Disagreements identified and plausibly explained | Disagreements noted but unexplained | "Experimental error", or agreement claimed without comparison | 2 |
| Communication, reproducibility, safety documentation | 15% | Anyone could repeat it from the report; safety box complete; limitations honest; figures captioned with units | Clear report, code and data included | Report incomplete or hard to follow | Missing code/data or missing safety box | Safety box present (hard requirement) |

## Passing Phase 00

Pass requires: exam Parts A–D each at proficient level or above for the competencies they tag, **and** the mini-project at or above the minimum in every row with a weighted score of 3.0 or more. Report the outcome per competency as *practice completed*, *self-assessed mastery*, or *externally reviewed mastery* (the last only when a peer, mentor, or club reviewer has graded the report against this rubric). None of these is a credential.
