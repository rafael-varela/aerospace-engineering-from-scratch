# Phase 08 parameterized validation cases

These are unseen inputs for the computational problems, so answers cannot be copied. Each case is a JSON file `{"lesson": "P08.NN", "seed": int, "inputs": {...}, "expected": {...}, "tolerance": {...}}`. `generate.py` generates new cases from the reference solutions, and `run.py` checks a learner's implementation against them.

Expected values come from a closed form wherever the fluid mechanics has one. Otherwise they come from an independent oracle:
- `scipy.integrate.solve_ivp` and `solve_bvp` on the governing ODE, written separately from the reference solution;
- `scipy.integrate.quad` for integral balances;
- `scipy.optimize.brentq` for implicit relations;
- SymPy's exact `nullspace` for dimension matrices;
- `numpy.linalg.eig` for stability problems;
- for the CFD case, a reference solution computed once on the same grid by separate code.

Every case also contains a *limit* or a *conservation residual* that its physics must reproduce exactly: zero velocity, zero viscosity, an isothermal layer, a closed body with zero drag, a steady flow whose three lines coincide, a mass or momentum balance that closes, or a result unchanged by rescaling units. An implementation tuned to return the right number at the generated point therefore cannot pass.

Tolerances are set so that a correct from-scratch implementation passes and a specific, named physics or convention error fails. Each bullet states both, with the size of the error taken from a probe run on the exam's own numbers. Anything random inside a case (geometry, flow conditions, noise added to a synthetic record, a synthetic turbulence signal) is generated from the case's seed with `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)`. Statistical tolerances are stated with the sample count that justifies them. No case contains a measurement. Inputs that imitate a measurement (a hot-wire record, a velocity profile, a draining-bottle record) are labelled `"synthetic": true` in the JSON, and `run.py` prints that label with the result. Published values used as expected outputs (the ISA anchors, the Blasius constants, the Orr–Sommerfeld critical point, the Ghia et al. table) are labelled `"published": true`.

Cases planned, one family per computational lesson:

- `p08-01-atmosphere-*.json` (P08.01): seeded geopotential altitudes in 0–47 km (always including one point in each layer and one on each layer boundary), a seeded manometer (fluid densities, a reading) and a seeded buoyant body. Outputs: T, p, ρ, μ and ν at each altitude, the manometer Δp and the gross lift.
  - **Oracle:** the closed-form layer laws, and independently `solve_ivp` on dp/dz = −pg₀/(RT(z)) with rtol 10⁻¹²; the ISA anchor values (published) at 11, 20, 32 and 47 km.
  - **Limit:** the isothermal layer satisfies p(z₂)/p(z₁) = exp(−g₀Δz/(RT)) exactly; continuity of p across every layer boundary to 10⁻¹²; T = 288.15 K gives μ₀ = 1.7894 × 10⁻⁵ Pa s.
  - **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁹ against the ODE oracle and 10⁻⁵ against the published anchors (the closed forms with the stated constants differ from the anchors by up to 2 × 10⁻⁶).
  - **Errors caught:** geometric altitude used as geopotential (+0.30% in p at 11 km); R rounded to 287 (−0.13% at 47 km); Sutherland evaluated with T in °C (a factor of 130 at 3000 m); a layer that restarts from p₀ instead of from the previous layer's top.
- `p08-02-kinematics-*.json` (P08.02): a seeded 2-D velocity field from a family of analytic unsteady fields (uniform plus oscillating cross-flow, a translating vortex, a pulsating stagnation flow), a release point, a release interval and an observation time. Outputs: points on the streamline, the pathline and the streakline at that time, the material acceleration, and the strain-rate and rotation tensors at seeded points.
  - **Oracle:** closed-form pathlines and streaklines where the field has them (the exam's question 6 field); otherwise `solve_ivp` at rtol 10⁻¹⁰; SymPy differentiation for D/Dt and the velocity gradient.
  - **Limit:** with the time dependence switched off (ω = 0) the three lines coincide to 10⁻⁸ of their length; the rotation tensor of an irrotational field is zero to 10⁻¹².
  - **Tolerance:** points atol 10⁻⁶ of the domain size.
  - **Errors caught:** a streakline traced by releasing every particle at t = 0, which collapses it onto the pathline (in the exam's field the point at x = 2 m at t = 3 s comes out at y = 0.637 m instead of 0); the convective term dropped from D/Dt (zero instead of (10, 5) m/s² in the stagnation flow); the vorticity reported as the rotation rate (a factor of 2).
- `p08-03-control-volume-*.json` (P08.03): a seeded jet engine (air and fuel flows, exit velocity, exit area and pressure, flight speed), a rocket nozzle (mass flow, exit conditions, ambient pressure) and a deflector or vane, fixed or moving. Outputs: thrust, ram drag, specific impulse, deflector forces and power, and the mass and momentum residuals of the control volume.
  - **Oracle:** the closed-form balances, evaluated with exact rational arithmetic (`fractions.Fraction`) for rational inputs.
  - **Limit:** the mass and momentum residuals are zero to round-off (10⁻¹² of the largest flux); a vane moving at the jet speed carries no force; p_e = p_a gives zero pressure thrust.
  - **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰.
  - **Errors caught:** the pressure thrust dropped (15 500 N instead of 10 434 N at sea level for the exam's rocket); the ram drag dropped in flight (11 220 N instead of 7220 N); the absolute instead of the relative velocity used in the moving control volume's mass flux (20.4 instead of 18.55 kg/s).
- `p08-04-pitot-static-*.json` (P08.04): a seeded pressure altitude, true airspeed below M = 0.3, static-port pressure coefficient, duct geometry, loss coefficients and fan efficiency. Outputs: dynamic pressure, IAS, EAS, the indicated airspeed and altitude errors from the port, and the fan pressure rise and shaft power.
  - **Oracle:** incompressible Bernoulli and the steady-flow energy equation in closed form, with the ISA of the P08.01 oracle.
  - **Limit:** at sea level IAS = EAS = TAS; C_p = 0 gives no error; K = 0 and η = 1 give the ideal fan.
  - **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰.
  - **Errors caught:** IAS computed with the local instead of the sea-level density (60.0 instead of 51.7 m/s at the exam's cruise); the static error applied with the wrong sign (an airspeed 2.5% low instead of high); the outlet kinetic energy left out of the fan rise (723 instead of 963 Pa in the exam's duct).
- `p08-05-buckingham-pi-*.json` (P08.05): a seeded physical problem given as a list of 4–8 variables with their dimensions in M, L, T and Θ (including deliberately degenerate sets in which two dimensions only appear together), plus a model scale and tunnel conditions. Outputs: the rank, a set of independent groups, and the model speed, pressure or temperature that matches a chosen pair of groups (or the statement that no condition can).
  - **Oracle:** SymPy's exact `nullspace` and rank, and the SVD rank with a relative threshold of 10⁻¹⁰; any learner basis is accepted if it spans the same space (checked by the rank of the stacked bases).
  - **Limit:** a variable set with one variable per dimension gives no group; every returned group has zero dimension exactly.
  - **Tolerance:** the rank and the spanned space must match exactly; matching conditions rtol 10⁻⁹.
  - **Errors caught:** a group count taken as n minus the number of dimensions, which fails the degenerate sets; groups that are not independent (rank of the basis below n − r); μ assumed to scale with pressure in a pressurised tunnel (which would make the required pressure infinite).
- `p08-06-exact-solutions-*.json` (P08.06): a seeded Couette–Poiseuille gap (h, U, dp/dx, μ), a pipe (R, G, μ, ρ), and a Stokes first problem (U, ν, t). Outputs: profiles at seeded points, flow rates, wall shear stresses, the reverse-flow threshold, the penetration depth, and the learner's finite-difference solution of Stokes's problem on two grids.
  - **Oracle:** the closed forms; erfc from `scipy.special`.
  - **Limit:** dp/dx = 0 gives linear Couette; U = 0 gives the symmetric parabola; residuals of the momentum equation and the boundary conditions, evaluated on the returned profiles by finite differences, are zero to 10⁻⁸.
  - **Tolerance:** closed forms rtol 10⁻¹⁰. The finite-difference Stokes solution must agree to 10⁻³ relative at the finer grid, and the error ratio between grids must match the scheme's order (4 ± 0.3 for second order). A Poiseuille-only test cannot measure the order (exam question 10(d)), which is why Stokes's problem is the convergence case.
  - **Errors caught:** the h³/(12μ) of plane flow used in a pipe (or the pipe's R⁴/8 in a plane gap); f = 16/Re (Fanning) reported as Darcy (a factor of 4).
- `p08-07-feed-line-*.json` (P08.07): a seeded line (diameter, length, roughness), fluid (density and viscosity labelled typical), mass flow (spanning laminar, transitional-excluded and turbulent cases), a list of minor-loss coefficients, an elevation, an effective gravity and the injector requirement. Outputs: Re, f by Colebrook and by Haaland, the friction and minor losses, and the tank pressure.
  - **Oracle:** Colebrook solved by `brentq` on 1/√f (independently of the learner's Newton iteration); 64/Re for laminar cases.
  - **Limit:** zero flow gives zero loss and a hydrostatic tank pressure; ε → 0 reproduces the smooth-pipe Colebrook value.
  - **Tolerance:** Colebrook rtol 10⁻¹⁰, and Newton must converge in at most 8 iterations from f = 0.02 (it takes 4 in the exam's line). Haaland within 1.5% of Colebrook: its largest deviation over 4 × 10³ < Re < 10⁸ and 0 ≤ ε/D ≤ 0.05 is 1.4% in a probe grid of 420 points. Losses and tank pressure rtol 10⁻⁹.
  - **Errors caught:** a natural logarithm in place of log₁₀ (f = 0.0041 instead of 0.0183, a factor of 4.5); a turbulent formula applied at Re < 2000 (no laminar cases may be solved by Colebrook); the elevation head added with the wrong sign for a tank above the engine.
- `p08-08-potential-flow-*.json` (P08.08): seeded superpositions (uniform flow plus 1–4 sources, sinks, doublets and vortices, including the lifting cylinder and the Rankine oval) and a body contour where one exists. Outputs: the complex velocity at seeded points, stagnation points, the body's dimensions, the surface C_p, and the lift and drag by pressure integration and by contour integration.
  - **Oracle:** the analytic complex potential differentiated by SymPy; `brentq` for the oval's half-width; Blasius's theorem by trapezoidal contour integration on 4000 points (spectrally accurate for a periodic integrand).
  - **Limit:** the normal velocity on the body is below 10⁻¹⁰ U; the drag of every closed body is zero to 10⁻¹⁰ ρU²R; the lift equals ρUΓ to 10⁻¹⁰.
  - **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁹ on velocities and dimensions.
  - **Errors caught:** a vortex with the wrong sense (lift −1513 instead of +1513 N/m in the exam's cylinder, and the stagnation points on the upper surface); the oval's half-width solved with π − arctan instead of π/2 − arctan; the doublet strength taken as UR² instead of 2πUR² in a real-potential convention (the body is then not a circle of radius R).
- `p08-09-circulation-*.json` (P08.09): a seeded vortex (free, Rankine, Lamb–Oseen with ν and t), a seeded material loop (circle or polygon, enclosing or not enclosing the axis), and a horseshoe or straight vortex segment with field points. Outputs: the circulation round the loop at seeded times (with the loop advected), the vorticity flux through it, and the Biot–Savart induced velocity.
  - **Oracle:** the analytic circulation of each vortex inside a circle; Stokes's theorem by `quad` of the vorticity over the loop's area; the closed-form segment law Γ(cos α₁ − cos α₂)/(4πh).
  - **Limit:** in inviscid flow the circulation of an advected loop is constant to the integrator's tolerance; a loop that does not enclose the axis of a free vortex has zero circulation; an infinite line gives Γ/(2πh).
  - **Tolerance:** circulation rtol 10⁻⁶ (the loop is resolved by at least 2000 points, and the advection uses rtol 10⁻¹⁰); Lamb–Oseen decay rtol 10⁻⁶.
  - **Errors caught:** a loop integral that omits the closing segment; the semi-infinite vortex given as Γ/(2πh) (doubling the exam's horseshoe downwash to 1.26 m/s by accident); the Lamb–Oseen core taken as √(νt) instead of √(4νt).
- `p08-10-blasius-*.json` (P08.10): seeded η_max, step counts and flow conditions (U, ν, x). Outputs: f''(0) by shooting, δ*, θ, H, η₉₉, and the dimensional δ₉₉, δ*, θ, c_f and τ_w at the stations.
  - **Oracle:** `solve_bvp` at tol 10⁻¹⁰; the published constants (f''(0) = 0.332057, δ* = 1.7208, θ = 0.6641).
  - **Limit:** θ-coefficient = 2f''(0) (the momentum integral) to 10⁻⁶; doubling U and ν together leaves every dimensionless output unchanged.
  - **Tolerance:** f''(0) atol 2 × 10⁻⁶ against the published value and 10⁻⁸ against the oracle at n ≥ 1000; the RK4 error ratio between n and 2n must give an order of 4 ± 0.3 (3.84 at n = 50 to 100 in the exam's question 22).
  - **Errors caught:** the similarity variable scaled with √(U/(2νx)), which gives the equation f''' + ff'' = 0 with f''(0) = 0.4696, a factor √2; δ₉₉ reported as δ*.
- `p08-11-thwaites-*.json` (P08.11): a seeded outer velocity U_e(x) from a family (flat plate, stagnation point, Howarth's linear deceleration, a cylinder, a Falkner–Skan wedge with m from the seed, and a piecewise accelerating–decelerating aerofoil-like distribution), ν and a station list. Outputs: θ, λ, H, c_f at the stations and the separation point.
  - **Oracle:** the closed-form Thwaites integrals where they exist (flat plate, stagnation point, Howarth, Falkner–Skan); `quad` otherwise; the separation point by `brentq` on λ = −0.090; for comparison only (not for grading), `solve_bvp` Falkner–Skan profiles.
  - **Limit:** a flat plate gives λ = 0 and θ = 0.6708√(νx/U); a stagnation point gives θ independent of x.
  - **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁸ against the oracle; separation points atol 10⁻⁶ of the length.
  - **Errors caught:** the exponents of U_e swapped (U_e⁶ inside the integral); λ with the wrong sign of dU_e/dx (which predicts no separation in Howarth's flow, instead of x/L = 0.123); the coefficient 0.441 used in place of the stated 0.45 (−1.0% in θ: allowed only if the lesson states it; the case file carries the coefficient as an input).
- `p08-12-stability-*.json` (P08.12): a seeded plane Poiseuille case (α in 0.8–1.2 and Re in 4000–10 000), and a seeded flat-plate run (U, ν, length, free-stream turbulence level). Outputs: the least stable eigenvalue c, its growth rate, the neutral Re at the given α, the N-factor along the plate from the supplied envelope fit (labelled approximate), and the transition position for N from Mack's relation.
  - **Oracle:** an independent Chebyshev collocation solver on 120 points (`numpy.linalg.eig` of the generalised problem by separate code); the published critical point (Re = 5772.22, α = 1.02056); the closed-form envelope.
  - **Limit:** at the published critical point the growth rate is zero to 10⁻⁶ (the exam's probe on 80 points gives c_i = −3.6 × 10⁻⁹); increasing the collocation count from 80 to 120 changes c by less than 10⁻⁸.
  - **Tolerance:** c atol 10⁻⁶; neutral Re rtol 10⁻⁴; N rtol 10⁻¹⁰ (it is a closed form); transition Re_x rtol 10⁻⁸.
  - **Errors caught:** clamped boundary conditions imposed on φ only and not φ' (spurious modes and a wrong critical Re); a Reynolds number based on the mean velocity and the full height used as if it were the centreline–half-width value (7696 against 5772); ln confused with log₁₀ in Mack's relation.
- `p08-13-turbulence-stats-*.json` (P08.13): a seeded synthetic velocity record (`"synthetic": true`) generated with a von Kármán model spectrum of known variance and integral scale, a mean velocity, a sampling rate and a record length. Outputs: mean, variance, skewness, flatness, the autocorrelation, the integral time and length scales, the spectrum, a −5/3 fit and the Kolmogorov scales from a stated ε estimate.
  - **Oracle:** the generating parameters (variance, integral scale and the spectrum level are known by construction).
  - **Limit:** a record of pure Gaussian white noise returns an integral time of one sample step and a flat spectrum; scaling every velocity by a factor scales the variance by its square and leaves the integral time unchanged.
  - **Tolerance:** statistical, with the record length set from the formula of the exam's question 17(d). The mean is checked within four standard uncertainties u'√(2T/T_rec); the variance within four standard deviations of the variance estimate, taken from seeded replicates in the generator (for an exponential autocorrelation the formula √(2T/T_rec) was reproduced to 5% by 200 seeded records; the von Kármán signal has its own value, which the generator stores). The integral time, estimated by integrating the autocorrelation to its first zero crossing, is checked within 25%: in 300 seeded records of about 13 000 integral times each, that estimator had a standard deviation of 6.4% and a bias of +5%. The −5/3 slope must come within ±0.1 when fitted over the generator's inertial range.
  - **Errors caught:** the autocorrelation integrated over half the record without a cut-off (in the same 300 records the estimate then averaged 0.28 of the true value with a standard deviation of 60%); a two-sided spectrum reported as one-sided (a factor of 2 in E(0)); the mean not removed before the autocorrelation.
- `p08-14-turbulent-bl-*.json` (P08.14): seeded flat-plate conditions (U, ν, length, transition Re_x), and a seeded synthetic mean profile (`"synthetic": true`) from Spalding's law with known u_τ and noise. Outputs: c_f and C_F by the stated correlations, the mixed-plate constant A and C_F, u_τ, ν/u_τ, δ, Re_τ, first-cell heights, u_τ by a Clauser fit, and Spalding's u⁺ at seeded y⁺. The synthetic profile follows the log law for 50 < y⁺ < 0.2δ⁺ and Spalding's law below, so the Clauser oracle is exact on the log-region points.
  - **Oracle:** the correlations in closed form; `brentq` on Spalding's law; the generating u_τ.
  - **Limit:** Re_tr → Re_L gives the all-laminar C_F; Re_tr → 0 gives the all-turbulent one; Spalding's law tends to u⁺ = y⁺ at small y⁺ and to the log law at large y⁺.
  - **Tolerance:** closed forms rtol 10⁻¹⁰; the Clauser u_τ within 0.2% (noise of 0.05 m/s at 20 log-region points gave a standard deviation of 0.025% in u_τ over 2000 seeded replicates).
  - **Errors caught:** the Clauser fit run on points outside the log region (fitting a Spalding profile from y⁺ = 5 biased u_τ by −3.4% in the probe); A derived with the local laminar 0.664 instead of the average 1.328 (A = 2.21 × 10³ instead of 1.74 × 10³); log₁₀ and ln swapped in the log law.
- `p08-15-mixing-length-*.json` (P08.15): a seeded channel Re_τ (180–5000), van Driest constant and κ, and seeded log-layer conditions (u_τ, y). Outputs: the mixing-length velocity profile, centreline and bulk u⁺, c_f, the implied B, and the k–ε equilibrium values k, ε and ν_t with the κ implied by the constants.
  - **Oracle:** `solve_bvp` (or `quad` of the explicit gradient) on the same closure written independently; closed forms for the equilibrium values.
  - **Limit:** the total shear stress reconstructed from the returned profile equals 1 − y/h to 10⁻⁸; with A⁺ → 0 the profile in the overlap is the pure log law with B determined by the sublayer match; ν_t = κu_τy equals C_μk²/ε exactly.
  - **Tolerance:** u⁺ rtol 10⁻⁶ at 20 001 points; equilibrium values rtol 10⁻¹².
  - **Errors caught:** the damping applied to ν_t instead of ℓ (or squared twice); the turbulent stress set equal to τ_w everywhere instead of 1 − y/h (the centreline u⁺ is then 22.13 instead of 20.58 at Re_τ = 1000, above the log law's 21.85); κ² = σ_ε√C_μ(C₂ − C₁) inverted wrongly.
- `p08-16-drag-*.json` (P08.16): seeded spheres (diameter, density, fluid) spanning Re from 0.1 to 10⁶, cylinders with a Strouhal number, and a slender body with frontal and wetted areas. Outputs: c_d by Schiller–Naumann and by Morrison (labelled correlations), terminal velocities by iteration, shedding frequencies, drag per length, and the friction share of a body's drag.
  - **Oracle:** the correlations in closed form; terminal velocity by `brentq` on the force balance.
  - **Limit:** Re → 0 gives Stokes's law (Schiller–Naumann tends to 24/Re); a body with the fluid's own density has zero terminal velocity; the force balance residual is below 10⁻¹⁰ of the weight.
  - **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻⁹. Schiller–Naumann is used only below Re = 800: there it differs from Morrison by up to 14%, and the case checks each correlation against itself, never one against the other.
  - **Errors caught:** buoyancy left out (which matters for light spheres in water); the drag crisis missed by using Schiller–Naumann's constant 0.44 above Re = 2.5 × 10⁵ (0.44 instead of 0.17 at 3 × 10⁵); a radius used for the diameter in Re or in St.
- `p08-17-compressible-*.json` (P08.17): seeded ISA altitudes and Mach numbers (0.05–3), and seeded pitot readings. Outputs: a, isentropic ratios, q_c, CAS, EAS, the incompressible error, the Rayleigh pitot ratio, and the Mach number inverted from a pitot reading, subsonic or supersonic as the reading requires.
  - **Oracle:** closed forms; `brentq` for the inversion of the Rayleigh formula, written independently of the learner's Newton iteration.
  - **Limit:** at M = 1 the Rayleigh and isentropic ratios agree to 10⁻¹²; at sea level CAS = EAS; M → 0 gives q_c/q → 1.
  - **Tolerance:** rtol 10⁻¹⁰.
  - **Errors caught:** the subsonic formula used above M = 1 (M = 1.63 instead of 1.74 for the exam's reading); γ/(γ − 1) replaced by 1/(γ − 1) in the isentropic pressure ratio; CAS computed with the local static pressure instead of p₀.
- `p08-18-cavity-*.json` (P08.18): a seeded Reynolds number in 1–400, a seeded grid (16–48 intervals) and a seeded set of physical scales (lid speed, side length, viscosity) that give that Reynolds number. Outputs: the steady ψ field, ψ_min and its location, the centreline velocity profiles, the residual of the steady vorticity equation, the observed order from three grids, and the comparison with the Ghia et al. table (labelled published) at Re = 100.
  - **Oracle:** a reference stream function–vorticity solution on the same grid, computed once by separate code and stored in the case; the published Ghia et al. Re = 100 centreline table.
  - **Limit:** dimensionless results depend only on Re, so two cases with different U, L and ν but the same Re agree to 10⁻¹⁰. As Re → 0 the solution tends to the Stokes-flow solution, which is exactly symmetric about x = 0.5; a low-Re case checks that the asymmetry falls in proportion to Re. ψ = 0 on all four walls to the solver tolerance. The flux between the floor and the vortex centre, and between the centre and the lid, are equal and opposite.
  - **Tolerance:**
    - against the same-grid reference, ψ atol 10⁻⁷, so that two correct implementations of the same scheme agree and a scheme change is caught;
    - observed order 2 ± 0.2 from the three grids (2.04 for ψ_min from 32, 64 and 128 intervals in the exam's question 24);
    - at Re = 100 on 32 intervals, ψ_min within 3% of the published −0.103423 (the exam's solver: −0.10043, −2.9%), and on 64 intervals within 1%.
  - **Errors caught:**
    - the lid term of Thom's formula with the wrong sign (the vortex then turns the wrong way);
    - a first-order wall-vorticity formula (observed order near 1);
    - a "steady state" stopped before convergence (the vorticity residual is checked to 10⁻⁸).
  - **Not a check:** the discrete divergence. It is zero identically for this formulation, so it is reported but never used as a mass check.

The cases are written once the reference solutions for lessons 01–18 are merged; `generate.py` then produces them deterministically from a seed. Lesson 19 is assessed through its physical report, which the rubric grades. Its reduction (the √h fit with a free offset, C_d with both uncertainty types, the manometer airspeeds with the resolution and angle terms) is covered by the exam's question 23 and needs no seeded case, since its unseen input is the learner's own bottle and fan. A seeded synthetic draining record for practice is part of the kit (`projects/p08-flow-pressure/data/synthetic/`).
