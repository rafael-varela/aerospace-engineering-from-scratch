# Phase 01 Grading rubric

Used for the Phase 01 mini-project and for grading the exam's Part D. **The mini-project is the wind-tunnel drag-polar fit report from P01.17** (Orthogonality, Least Squares & QR): a data-based investigation that fits C_D = C_D0 + K C_L² to published wind-tunnel measurements with a least-squares solver written from scratch, reports the fitted parameters with their uncertainty, and checks the fit against the limiting cases from lessons 02 and 13–18. It is chosen over the P01.16 principal-axes analysis because it exercises more of the phase (calculus from lesson 02, elimination and conditioning from lessons 13 and 18, projection and QR from lesson 17) and because it works on authentic measurements rather than on numbers the learner invented. Phase 01 has no physical experiment, so the Phase 00 row on experiment design is replaced by a row on data handling, and the safety hard requirement is replaced by a **data-provenance** hard requirement: the report must say where the data came from, under what conditions it was taken (Reynolds number, Mach number, model, tunnel corrections if stated), and that the learner did not measure it. Weights are proposed and will be calibrated in pilots. **Minimum thresholds apply in the mathematics rows: presentation quality never compensates for a wrong derivation or for a result that the code does not reproduce.**

| Dimension | Weight | 4 (exemplary) | 3 (proficient) | 2 (developing) | 1 (insufficient) | Minimum |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correct mathematics, derivation, assumptions | 25% | Normal equations derived from the projection condition A^T(b − Aθ) = 0; the quadratic polar model stated with its assumptions (attached flow, linear range, no Mach effects) and the C_L range where it holds; (L/D)_max and C_L* derived from the fit, not read off a plot | Derivation correct; model and its range of validity stated | A step asserted rather than derived, or an assumption left implicit | Normal equations wrong, or the polar fitted outside its range without comment | 3 |
| Calculation and numerical correctness | 20% | Solver written from scratch (elimination or QR) and checked against `numpy.linalg.lstsq`; every number in the report reproduced by the submitted code; residuals orthogonal to the columns to round-off; significant figures match the uncertainty | Correct results, code runs and agrees with the library | Results plausible but code does not reproduce them, or disagrees with the library without explanation | Arithmetic, unit or indexing errors in the fit | 2 |
| Data handling and model selection | 20% | Provenance box complete; raw data kept unchanged and the reduction scripted; points outside the quadratic range (stall, high C_L) identified and excluded with a stated criterion; a competing model (for example a cubic term or a C_L-offset term) fitted and rejected or accepted on evidence | Provenance stated; data reduced reproducibly; range of the fit justified | Data partly transcribed by hand, or the range chosen by eye without a criterion | No provenance, or data edited to improve the fit | 2 |
| Verification, validation, sensitivity | 20% | Fit checked against limiting cases (C_D → C_D0 at C_L = 0, equal drag split at C_L*); condition number of A^T A reported and compared with the QR route; parameter uncertainty from the residual and (A^T A)⁻¹; a leave-one-out or perturbed-input test done and interpreted | Limiting cases checked; conditioning or uncertainty reported | Fit shown but not validated against anything outside itself | Agreement claimed without a check, or "the fit looks good" | 2 |
| Communication, reproducibility, honesty about limits | 15% | Anyone could reproduce the fit from the report and repository; figures captioned with units and the data source; the report says what the model cannot predict (stall, compressibility) and never presents a fitted curve as a measurement | Clear report, code and data included, limits stated | Report incomplete or hard to follow; limits implicit | Missing code or data, or a fitted curve presented as measured | Provenance box present (hard requirement) |

## Passing Phase 01

Pass requires: exam Parts A–D each at proficient level or above for the competencies they tag, **and** the mini-project at or above the minimum in every row with a weighted score of 3.0 or more. Report the outcome per competency as *practice completed*, *self-assessed mastery*, or *externally reviewed mastery* (the last only when a peer, mentor, or club reviewer has graded the report against this rubric). None of these is a credential.
