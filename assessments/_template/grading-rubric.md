# Phase XX  Grading rubric

Used for the phase mini-project and for capstone-style projects. Weights are proposed and will be calibrated in pilots. **Minimum thresholds apply in the physics and safety rows: presentation quality never compensates for wrong physics or unsafe method.**

| Dimension | Weight | 4 (exemplary) | 3 (proficient) | 2 (developing) | 1 (insufficient) | Minimum |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correct physics, derivation, assumptions | 25% | Every assumption named where used; limits of validity stated and tested | Derivation correct; assumptions listed | Minor errors; assumptions partly implicit | Wrong governing equations or unstated critical assumptions | 3 |
| Calculation and numerical correctness | 20% | Verified against analytic case, convergence shown, units checked | Correct results; some verification | Results plausible but unverified | Incorrect or unit-inconsistent | 2 |
| Experiment design, data quality, uncertainty | 20% | Prediction recorded before measuring; calibration; repeated trials; uncertainty propagated | Calibrated, uncertainty estimated | Single trial or no calibration | No uncertainty or data not reproducible | 2 |
| Verification, validation, discrepancy investigation | 20% | Discrepancies explained with evidence and a follow-up test | Discrepancies identified and plausibly explained | Discrepancies noted but unexplained | Agreement claimed without comparison | 2 |
| Communication, reproducibility, safety documentation | 15% | Anyone could reproduce it; safety box complete; limitations honest | Clear report, code and data included | Report incomplete or hard to follow | Missing code/data or missing safety box | Safety box present (hard requirement) |

For simulation-only or data-only projects, replace the experiment row with "numerical or data-based validation" and state explicitly in the report that no physical measurement was made.

## The four competencies

Report each separately as *practice completed*, *self-assessed mastery*, or *externally reviewed mastery*:

1. Mathematical and physical reasoning
2. Analytical problem solving
3. Computational competence
4. Engineering interpretation
