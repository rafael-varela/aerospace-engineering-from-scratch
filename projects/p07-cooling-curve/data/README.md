# Data folder: p07-cooling-curve

- `prediction.md`: the bar's measured dimensions and mass with uncertainties, the room temperature, V, A, L_c and ρcV, h_conv, h_rad and h at θ = 14 K, the Biot number, the predicted τ ± u with the budget term by term, and the predicted τ for each third of the run (2–20, 20–40, 40–60 min). Dated, written before the bar goes into the water, never edited afterwards.
- `calibration.csv`: columns `date,item,reference,reading,unit,note`, for example the side-by-side readings of the probe and the room thermometer (offset and reading uncertainty), the probe's step response in lukewarm water, the measured water temperature before and after the warm-up (`water_start,probe thermometer,,57.4,C,limit 60 C`), and the D, L and mass readings.
- `raw/YYYY-MM-DD_<tier>_cooling.csv`: columns `run,t_s,T_C,note`, one row per reading of the bar's surface temperature, t from the moment the probe touched the bar. Note any disturbance (door, person, heating) with its time.
- `raw/YYYY-MM-DD_<tier>_ambient.csv`: columns `run,t_s,T_air_C,note`, the room thermometer at 0, 30 and 60 min (and any extra readings).
- `reduced/`: outputs of the analysis script (the fit tables and the plots).

Never edit a raw file. Fix mistakes in the analysis script and say so in the report.

## SYNTHETIC data

`synthetic/SYNTHETIC_seed715_cooling.csv` and `synthetic/SYNTHETIC_seed715_ambient.csv` are **SYNTHETIC**: they were generated by `make_synthetic_cooling_data(seed=715)` (written by `write_synthetic_csv`) in the lesson's `solution.py` (the same generator is given in `starter/main.py`) and are **not measurements**. The first lines of each file say so. They describe the lesson's bar (25.4 mm × 50 mm 6061 aluminium nominal, hung horizontally in still air) read every 15 s for 60 min by a probe of 0.1 °C resolution taped to its surface. The generator's hidden "truth" differs from the nominal inputs on purpose (convection 1.12 times the Churchill–Chu correlation, emittance 0.12 instead of 0.09, a bar 50.3 mm long, room air at 21.3 °C, the bar at 49.6 °C when the probe is attached, a probe lag of 20 s starting from room temperature) and adds normal reading noise of 0.04 °C before rounding every reading to 0.1 °C.

- `SYNTHETIC_seed715_cooling.csv`: columns `t_s,T_C`, 241 readings from 0 to 3600 s.
- `SYNTHETIC_seed715_ambient.csv`: columns `t_s,T_air_C`, the room at 0, 1800 and 3600 s.

Read them with `read_csv` from the lesson code (lines starting with `#` are comments). To make your own set, call `write_synthetic_csv(out_dir, seed=<your seed>)`; keep **SYNTHETIC** in every file name, table caption and plot title derived from it, and never place synthetic files in `raw/`.

## prediction.md

`prediction.md` is the most important file in this folder. Write it before the bar goes into the water, with the date and time at the top; list every input with its source and uncertainty, the predicted τ ± u, the budget, and the windowed predictions; then do not edit it. If you later find an error in it, add a dated correction at the end and keep the original text: the report compares the measurement with what you predicted, not with what you would have predicted.
