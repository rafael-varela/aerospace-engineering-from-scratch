# Safety box: Pendulum system identification

Read this before buying anything and again before every session. If any stop criterion is met, stop, let the bob come to rest (or catch the string from the side), make the setup safe, and only then think about what happened.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hazards** | A swinging mass (the bob, or the phone used as the bob) striking a person, a pet, a screen, a window or a lamp. The phone or bob falling if the pouch, clip or string fails. The support, its clamp or the books weighting it falling off the table. Tripping over the string, the logging cable or the phone stand. A logging cable snagging the swing and pulling the pendulum or the computer. Standing on furniture to rig a high support. |
| **Energy limits** | String length ≤ 2.0 m. Bob mass ≤ 0.2 kg, including the phone, its pouch and clip when the phone is the bob, or the IMU and tape on the enhanced tier. Release amplitude ≤ 45°. At these limits the bob passes the bottom at 3.4 m/s carrying 1.15 J, and the string tension peaks at 3.1 N. Nothing heavier, longer or released higher; the pendulum is released from rest by hand and never pushed, spun, or driven by any motor or mechanism. |
| **Controls** | A soft, compact bob (rice bag, rubber ball, padded nut), or the phone zipped into a padded pouch with two attachment points so it cannot slip out or spin. Weigh the bob (or phone + pouch + clip) before the first swing. The whole swing arc plus 0.5 m on each side and below kept clear of people, pets, screens, glass and lamps; mark the arc's ends on the floor with tape; a folded blanket under the arc when the phone is the bob. Test the support before each session by hanging twice the bob's weight for one minute. Pinch the string in a clip at the pivot (no loose knots around a rod) and check it for fraying. Camera phone on a stand outside the arc. Any logging cable light, slack and tied along the string, routed away from the walkway. Release while standing beside the plane of the swing, never inside it; stop the swing by catching the string, not the bob, from the side. |
| **Supervision** | None needed for an adult. A child does this only with an adult handling the release and the catch. |
| **Location** | Indoors on a sturdy table or in a doorway with the swing plane clear; hard floor with a soft landing under the arc; good light for the camera. No rigging from ceilings, light fittings, curtain rails or shelves. |
| **Regulations** | None apply. |
| **Stop criteria** | Anyone or any pet enters the marked arc. The support moves, creaks, or the weighting shifts. The string frays, slips in the clip or stretches visibly. The bob or phone loosens in its pouch or on the string. The bob strikes anything. A cable tugs on the pendulum or the computer. The phone gets warm or its battery swells. You need to stand on a chair to reach the pivot. |
| **Disposal** | Rice or beans from a bob back to the kitchen if the bag stayed sealed; string, tape and stickers in household waste; an IMU breakout or wires to electronics recycling if you no longer need them. |
| **Not permitted in this project** | Bobs over 0.2 kg, including a phone whose total with its pouch exceeds 0.2 kg (use video instead); glass, ceramic or unpadded metal bobs; strings over 2.0 m; amplitudes over 45° or full loops; swinging over people, beds, desks with screens, or stairwells; batteries or power banks on the swinging bob; any powered drive of the pendulum (motors, magnets, shakers); combustion, pressure vessels, mains wiring. |

Safety is a hard requirement in the rubric: a report without a completed safety box is not graded.
