# Safety box: Airfoil lift and drag on a guarded-fan bench rig

Read this before buying anything and again before every session. If any stop criterion is met, switch the fan off **and unplug it**, wait until the blades have stopped, and only then touch anything or think about what happened.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hazards** | Fingers, hair, scarves, string, paper or straws drawn into a running fan through a damaged or missing grille. A fan that tips over or "walks" across the table while running. A model, sting, end plate or straw blown out of the jet into the face or into the fan. The mains cable tripped over or pulled into the fan. An overheating motor (a duct or box blocking its cooling vents). Cuts from a craft knife while shaping foam; burns and polystyrene fumes from a hot-wire foam cutter. Water from the manometer near a mains appliance. |
| **Energy limits** | **The fan is rated at most 50 W** (read its label; many large box fans draw more and are not allowed). Airspeed in the jet is about 3–5 m/s (dynamic pressure about 10 Pa, the force of a few grams on the model). **No propellers, drone motors, model-aircraft motors or unguarded blades, ever**, at any power. Mains voltage stays inside the fan's own, unmodified housing: no dimmers, no rewiring, no opened casings. The enhanced tier's electronics run from a computer's USB port (5 V). |
| **Controls** | **The fan is guarded on both sides: front and back grilles intact, undamaged and finger-proof** (a finger cannot pass any gap); check both grilles before every session. **The fan stands on a stable, level surface where it cannot tip or walk**: on its own flat base, weighted or taped down if it vibrates, away from the table edge. **Unplug the fan before adjusting the model, the angle, the balance, the straightener or anything within reach of the fan**; switching it off is not enough, because a switch can be knocked on. **No cutting tools near the running fan**: shape the foam elsewhere, or with the fan unplugged. **Hot-wire foam cutting only with adult supervision and good ventilation** (a window open, fumes not inhaled); otherwise use a craft knife and a sanding block, with the fan unplugged. The cable is routed behind the fan, along the floor or the table back, taped down, so it cannot be tripped over or pulled into the fan. Loose paper, hair (tied back), scarves, lanyards, string and straws are kept away from the intake side. The straw honeycomb is taped into its frame so no straw can leave it; the model, its end plates, the sting and the balance are secured so nothing can be blown into the fan (stand downstream of the model, never between the fan and the model). The duct or box never covers the fan's motor vents. Safety glasses whenever the fan runs. The manometer sits below and away from the fan, on a tray. |
| **Supervision** | An adult may work alone. A child or teenager works only with an adult present whenever the fan is plugged in; the adult does all hot-wire cutting. |
| **Location** | Indoors, on a sturdy table with room on all sides of the fan, in a room without pets or small children loose, away from curtains and loose paper, with a mains socket close enough that no extension lead crosses a walkway. Not on a bed, a sofa or a folding table. |
| **Regulations** | None apply beyond using the fan as its maker intended. Do not modify the appliance. |
| **Stop criteria** | **The fan rattles, wobbles, walks or changes sound.** **A grille is bent, cracked or loose, or anything touches a grille.** **The motor smells hot or the casing is hot to the touch.** The cable is damaged or warm. A straw, foam crumb or any part leaves the model, the straightener or the balance. The model flutters or hits its stop. Water spills near the fan or its cable. Anyone or any pet comes near the intake. Switch off, unplug, then investigate. |
| **Disposal** | Foam offcuts and dust: swept up (not vacuumed while the fan runs) and put in household waste, or taken to a polystyrene recycling point where one exists. Straws and card: recycling. Coloured manometer water: down the sink. The fan returns to household use unmodified. |
| **Not permitted in this project** | **Propellers, drones, model-aircraft or computer-fan motors without a full guard, or any unguarded blade**; fans above 50 W or with damaged, missing or removed grilles; modifying, opening or rewiring the fan or adding a speed controller; reaching near a plugged-in fan; cutting or sanding near a running fan; hot-wire cutting without an adult and ventilation; standing between the fan and the model while it runs; leaving a running fan unattended; using a fan to dry or blow anything else during the session. |

Safety is a hard requirement in the rubric: a report without a completed safety box is not graded.
