# Data folder: p10-airfoil-bench

All files are CSV with `#` comment lines first (what, when, how), then one header line. Read them with `read_bench_csv` or `load_bench_dataset` from the lesson code (`curriculum/phase-10/lesson-23-home-lab-airfoil-bench`). Your own files use the prefix `YYYY-MM-DD_<tier>`, for example `2026-11-02_recommended_sweep.csv`, and are loaded with `load_bench_dataset("data/raw", prefix="2026-11-02_recommended")`.

- `prediction.md`: the model's measured dimensions, room air, predicted airspeed and q, Re, the predicted CL and CD bands and scale changes at each planned α, the resolution-limited points, and the expected direction of the disagreement. Dated, written before the fan runs with the model, never edited afterwards.
- `raw/<prefix>_conditions.csv`: columns `quantity,value,unit,standard_uncertainty`. Rows `temperature` (°C), `pressure` (Pa), `relative_humidity` (0–1), `chord` (m), `span` (m), `incline` (deg, the manometer board), `lift_scale_resolution` (g), `drag_scale_resolution` (g).
- `raw/<prefix>_airspeed.csv`: columns `run,incline_deg,reading_zero_mm,reading_fan_mm`, one row per manometer reading pair (fan off, fan on), along the inclined tube. For an anemometer, record V in `reading_fan_mm` and say so in a comment line (and replace the manometer step of `reduce_bench` by `anemometer_q` in your copy).
- `raw/<prefix>_calibration.csv`: columns `kind,applied_g,reading_g`. `kind` is `drag_pivot` (horizontal loads over the pulley, read on the drag scale, up and down) or `lift_scale_check` (masses on the model base, read on the lift scale).
- `raw/<prefix>_sweep.csv`: columns `run,alpha_set_deg,lift_off_g,lift_on_g,drag_off_g,drag_on_g`, one row per angle per sweep; "off" and "on" are the fan states. The lift reading falls when the model lifts; the drag reading rises by the drag times the arm ratio.
- `raw/<prefix>_tare.csv`: columns `config,lift_off_g,lift_on_g,drag_off_g,drag_on_g`; `config` is `sting_only` (no model) or `model_alpha0` (model at the zero-lift setting).
- `reduced/`: outputs of the analysis (the CL/CD table, plots).

Never edit a raw file. Fix mistakes in the analysis script and say so in the report. Keep the photos; they are raw data too.

## SYNTHETIC data

The five files `synthetic/SYNTHETIC_seed230_conditions.csv`, `..._airspeed.csv`, `..._calibration.csv`, `..._sweep.csv` and `..._tare.csv` are **SYNTHETIC**: they were generated by `make_synthetic_bench_data(seed=230)` in the lesson's `solution.py` (the same generator is given in `starter/main.py`) and are **not measurements**. The first line of each file says so. They imitate the recommended tier (0.1 g lift scale, 0.01 g drag scale on a bell-crank, inclined water manometer) on the lesson's model: a foam NACA 0012, chord 100 mm, span 200 mm between end plates, in a jet of about 4 m/s (Re ≈ 2.6 × 10⁴).

The generator's "truth" is a set of **stated assumptions, not measured facts**: a lift slope of 0.060 per degree with a soft stall to CL_max ≈ 0.6 near 9–10° and a gentle fall beyond 11°; CD = 0.035 + 0.10 CL² + 6 × 10⁻⁴ max(|α| − 7°, 0)² (between about 0.035 and 0.1); a flow angularity of +0.6° in the jet; a fan speed drifting from 4.05 to 3.97 m/s with 1.5 % run-to-run unsteadiness; a sting with 0.005 N of drag and no lift; a true arm ratio of 2.04 against the nominal 2.0; a manometer incline of 10.15° recorded as 10.0°; reading noise of 0.3 mm on the manometer, 0.12 g on the lift scale and 0.04 g on the drag scale; room air at 21.0 °C, 100.8 kPa, 45 % relative humidity (recorded as 21.2 °C and 100.7 kPa). These were chosen to be plausible for a low-Reynolds-number model, so that the reduction has realistic problems to find; they are not a substitute for a measurement.

- `SYNTHETIC_seed230_conditions.csv`: the recorded room conditions, model dimensions, incline and scale resolutions.
- `SYNTHETIC_seed230_airspeed.csv`: six manometer reading pairs, three before and three after the sweep.
- `SYNTHETIC_seed230_calibration.csv`: the pivot calibration (0–10 g up and down) and the lift-scale check (0, 20, 50 g).
- `SYNTHETIC_seed230_sweep.csv`: two sweeps, −4° to 14° and back, in 2° steps.
- `SYNTHETIC_seed230_tare.csv`: three sting-only and three model-at-0° pairs.

To make your own set, call `write_synthetic_csv(out_dir, seed=<your seed>)`; keep **SYNTHETIC** in every file name, table caption and plot title derived from it, and never place synthetic files in `raw/`.
