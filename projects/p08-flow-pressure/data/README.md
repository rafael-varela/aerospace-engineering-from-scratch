# Data folder: p08-flow-pressure

- `prediction.md`: the bottle's inner diameter and the hole diameter with uncertainties and how they were measured; the assumed C_d; the predicted 200 → 30 mm draining time ± u with the budget term by term; the jet Reynolds numbers; the room's pressure and temperature and the air density; the manometer's resolution limit upright and tilted; the predicted Δh for each air source; the tilt decision. Dated, written before any water flows or any fan runs, never edited afterwards.
- `calibration.csv`: columns `date,item,reference,reading,unit,note`, for example each hole-diameter and bottle-diameter reading, the stopwatch check of the video timing, the repeated level readings that give u_h, the repeated manometer zero readings, the tilt measurements, the room pressure and temperature, and the water used (to show it stayed within 2 L).
- `raw/YYYY-MM-DD_<tier>_drain.csv`: columns `run,mark_mm,t_s,video,note`, one row per mark crossing; `mark_mm` is the ruler mark above the hole centre, `t_s` the video time at which the level crossed it.
- `raw/YYYY-MM-DD_<tier>_manometer.csv`: columns `source,setting,distance_mm,angle_deg,repeat,zero_left_mm,zero_right_mm,left_mm,right_mm,anemometer_mps,photo_zero,photo_flow,note`, one row per reading. The four readings are meniscus positions measured ALONG the tube (mm) on the ruler beside it; the pitot probe is on the left leg; `angle_deg` is the board's tilt from the horizontal (90 = upright); `anemometer_mps` is empty unless you have the enhanced tier.
- `reduced/`: outputs of the analysis script (the fit tables and the two plots).

Never edit a raw file. Fix mistakes in the analysis script and say so in the report. Keep the photos and videos; they are raw data too.

## SYNTHETIC data

`synthetic/SYNTHETIC_seed819_drain.csv` and `synthetic/SYNTHETIC_seed819_manometer.csv` are **SYNTHETIC**: they were generated by `make_synthetic_flow_data(seed=819)` in the lesson's `solution.py` (the same generator is given in `starter/main.py`; `write_synthetic_csv` reproduces the files byte for byte, which a test checks) and are **not measurements**. The first line of each file says so.

- `SYNTHETIC_seed819_drain.csv`: columns `run,mark_mm,t_s`; three runs, marks 200 to 30 mm in 10 mm steps. The bottle is nominally 90.0 ± 0.5 mm inside with a 4.0 ± 0.1 mm hole; the generator's hidden "truth" differs on purpose (D = 90.4 mm, d = 4.05 mm, C_d = 0.62, level 205 mm at t = 0) and adds normal noise of 0.08 s to each time and 0.5 mm to each level crossing.
- `SYNTHETIC_seed819_manometer.csv`: columns `source,setting,distance_mm,angle_deg,repeat,zero_left_mm,zero_right_mm,left_mm,right_mm,anemometer_mps`; a hair dryer on cool (true 12.0 and 8.5 m/s at 50 mm) and a guarded fan (true 3.6 and 2.8 m/s at 300 mm), each read upright (90°) and tilted at 10°, twice; room 101.0 kPa and 21 °C. Each meniscus reading has normal noise of 0.3 mm, each Δp a further 2 % for the stream's unsteadiness, and the anemometer column 3 % + 0.1 m/s.

Read them with `read_csv` from the lesson code (lines starting with `#` are comments). To make your own set, call `write_synthetic_csv(out_dir, seed=<your seed>)`; keep **SYNTHETIC** in every file name, table caption and plot title derived from it, and never place synthetic files in `raw/`.
