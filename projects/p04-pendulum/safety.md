# Safety box: Period of a string pendulum

Read this before buying anything and again before every session. If any stop criterion is met, stop, let the bob come to rest (or catch it from the side), make the setup safe, and only then think about what happened.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hazards** | A swinging mass striking a person, a pet, a screen, a window or a lamp. The support, its clamp or the books weighting it falling off the table. A string that snaps or slips, releasing the bob along its path. Tripping over the string, the tape measure or the phone stand. Standing on furniture to rig a high support. |
| **Energy limits** | Bob mass ≤ 0.2 kg. String length ≤ 2.0 m. Release amplitude ≤ 45°. At these limits the bob passes the bottom at 3.4 m/s carrying 1.15 J, about the energy of a tennis ball tossed gently, and the string tension peaks at 3.1 N. Nothing heavier, longer, or released higher; never pushed or spun. |
| **Controls** | A soft, compact bob (rice bag, rubber ball, padded nut). The whole swing arc plus 0.5 m on each side and below kept clear of people, pets, screens, glass and lamps; mark the arc's ends on the floor with tape. Test the support before each session by hanging twice the bob's weight for one minute. Pinch the string in a clip at the pivot (no loose knots around a rod) and check it for fraying. Phone on a stand outside the arc. Release the bob by hand while standing beside the plane of the swing, never inside it, and stop it by catching the string, not the bob, from the side. |
| **Supervision** | None needed for an adult. A child does this only with an adult handling the release and the catch. |
| **Location** | Indoors on a sturdy table or in a doorway with the swing plane clear; hard floor; good light for the camera. No rigging from ceilings, light fittings, curtain rails or shelves. |
| **Regulations** | None apply. |
| **Stop criteria** | Anyone or any pet enters the marked arc. The support moves, creaks, or the weighting shifts. The string frays, slips in the clip, or stretches visibly. The bob loosens on its string. The bob strikes anything. You need to stand on a chair to reach the pivot. |
| **Disposal** | Rice or beans from a bob back to the kitchen if the bag stayed sealed; string and tape in household waste. |
| **Not permitted in this project** | Bobs over 0.2 kg; glass, ceramic or unpadded metal bobs; strings over 2.0 m; amplitudes over 45° or full loops; swinging over people, beds, desks with screens, or stairwells; using the phone itself as a bob unless it is in a padded pouch and the total is within 0.2 kg; any powered drive of the pendulum. |

Safety is a hard requirement in the rubric: a report without a completed safety box is not graded.
