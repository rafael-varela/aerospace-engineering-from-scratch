# Safety box: Drop and toss light objects and measure their drag

Read this before buying anything and again before every session. If any stop criterion is met, stop, make the setup safe, and only then think about what happened.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hazards** | A falling or tossed object can hit an eye, a face, a phone, a pet or a child. A tossed ball bounces unpredictably off a wall or the floor and can reach a window, a screen or a lamp. Reaching a 2 m release height on tiptoe or on a chair is a fall hazard for *you*. A tape measure on the floor is a trip hazard. |
| **Energy limits** | Object mass ≤ 0.1 kg. Drop height ≤ 2.0 m (at most 2 J). Tosses by hand only, at a speed you can throw gently underarm, ≤ 8 m/s (a 2.7 g ball at 8 m/s carries 0.09 J; the 0.1 kg bean bag is not tossed, only dropped). Phone battery power only. No launcher, elastic, spring, blowgun or compressed air of any kind: a device that stores energy to launch a projectile is outside this project and outside this phase. |
| **Controls** | Soft, light objects only (a ping-pong ball, a bean bag). A towel or mat at the drop point and along the toss lane. A 1 m exclusion circle around the drop point and a clear lane of 4 m × 2 m for tosses, with nothing fragile (glass, screens, lamps) within 2 m of the lane's end. Safety glasses for everyone in the room during tosses. The phone on a stand 1.5 m to the side of the lane, never in it and never held over the drop zone. Tape measure fixed flat during marking and put away before any drop or toss. Release from a position you can reach standing on the floor; never from a chair, a ladder, a bed or a window. Windows and doors closed (draughts spoil the data and open windows invite a toss through them). |
| **Supervision** | None needed for adults. A child does this with an adult handling the release and the tosses. |
| **Location** | Indoors, a room with a hard floor, 2 m of clear height and a 4 m clear lane, or a corridor. Not a stairwell, not a balcony. Outdoors only in dead calm, with the same exclusion zones, and never near a road. |
| **Regulations** | None apply. |
| **Stop criteria** | Anyone or any pet enters the circle or the lane. A toss or a bounce heads toward a person, a window or a screen. You feel a draught (the data are spoiled; it is also a sign a door opened). Any floor or furniture damage. You feel unsteady reaching the release height. You are tired, rushed or distracted. |
| **Disposal** | None. The balls are reused in later projects. |
| **Not permitted in this project** | Metal, glass, hard-plastic or heavy objects (no ball bearings, golf balls, stones). Any launching device. Overarm throws or throws at people, pets, windows or screens. Dropping from windows, balconies, stairwells, chairs or ladders. Anyone standing under the drop point or at the end of the lane to "catch" the ball. |

Safety is a hard requirement in the rubric: a report without a completed safety box is not graded.
