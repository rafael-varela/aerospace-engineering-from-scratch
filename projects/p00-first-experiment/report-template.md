**Author:** · **Date:** · **Lesson:** P00.15 · **Equipment tier used:** · **Mode:** physical experiment

## Summary (5 sentences)
Question, method (video or stopwatch, frame rate), headline result (your g with uncertainty, or agreement at each height), whether the model held within uncertainty, main limitation.

## 1. Objective and requirements
Test t = √(2h/g) over 0.5–2.0 m; success criterion: normalized error below 2 at every height, or an identified cause where it is not.

## 2. Theory and assumptions
Free fall from rest with constant g and no drag; the terminal-speed argument for neglecting drag; the frame-quantization model for timing uncertainty.

## 3. Prediction (recorded before measurement)
Table: target height, predicted time, uncertainty, and the uncertainty budget (height, g, timing).

## 4. Apparatus, calibration, and safety
Phone and frame rate, release method, height measurement with its calibrated uncertainty (from p00-calibration), the completed safety box, deviations from the procedure.

## 5. Results
Raw frame tables (appendix) and per-height statistics; measured vs predicted plot with error bars; t² vs h fit and the resulting g ± U (k = 2).

## 6. Discussion
Normalized errors; ranked causes for any disagreement; the follow-up test and what it showed; how the uncertainty estimate held up.

## 7. Conclusions and redesign
What the evidence supports about the model and about your measurement; what you would change.

## 8. Reproducibility
Code commit, raw data files, video file names, BOM tier.
