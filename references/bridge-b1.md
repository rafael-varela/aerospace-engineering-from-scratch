# Reference map: Bridge B1: Precalculus & Trigonometry

Verified 2026-10-11 by loading the live pages; section numbers and titles are copied from the publishers' own chapter outlines or section pages. Every source here is **required-free** unless marked otherwise; sources are identified by an `@`-prefixed identifier and recorded in [sources.yaml](sources.yaml) with license, version, and caveats. **GAP** marks a lesson that no listed free source covers adequately: the lesson must carry original instruction there, and any "candidate" named is unverified until it is added to the manifest.

Conventions: OpenStax section URLs follow `https://openstax.org/books/<book-slug>/pages/<n>-<m>-<title-slug>`; a URL written as `.../pages/...` continues the last full URL above it. MIT OCW video URLs follow `https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/<course-slug>/resources/lecture-<n>-<slug>/`. OpenStax and MIT OCW are **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, Leishman is **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**: the curriculum links to them and writes its own explanations; nothing is copied or adapted.


## B1-01 Algebra & Units Refresher

Sources: `@openstax-college-algebra-2e`, `@openstax-precalculus-2e`

- CA 1.1 Real Numbers: Algebra Essentials — https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra-2e/pages/1-1-real-numbers-algebra-essentials — order of operations, properties of real numbers, evaluating/simplifying expressions.
- CA 1.2 Exponents and Scientific Notation — https://openstax.org/books/college-algebra-2e/pages/1-2-exponents-and-scientific-notation — exponent rules and scientific notation (the main tool for unit prefixes and order-of-magnitude checks).
- CA 1.3 Radicals and Rational Exponents — .../pages/1-3-radicals-and-rational-exponents — fractional powers (needed for drag/lift scaling laws).
- CA 1.5 Factoring Polynomials; 1.6 Rational Expressions — .../pages/1-5-factoring-polynomials, .../pages/1-6-rational-expressions — manipulating algebraic fractions.
- Units / dimensional analysis: **GAP** in the math books (Precalculus 2e has no units chapter). Suggest OpenStax College Physics 2e §1.2 "Physical Quantities and Units" and §1.3 "Accuracy, Precision, and Significant Figures" (not verified in this pass; same CC BY-NC-SA license).
